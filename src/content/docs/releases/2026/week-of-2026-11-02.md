Launch week. What reached Shuffle in the week starting Monday 2 November 2026.

## Paper folders

**Dock.** The dock and Apps got paper folders.

Folders look like paper folders, coloured by the kind of apps inside, with nine looks to choose from.

- Dock and Apps folders drawn as paper folders
- Folder colour follows the kind of apps inside
- 9 folder looks
- Dock right-click: pin beside a worded Close
- Faint line between pinned and open-only apps

[See Make it yours](https://shuffleplasma.com/yours)

## Control Center

**Status.** Control Center shows what is on at a glance.

Each pill says its state, Do Not Disturb has its own pill, and the volume and brightness bars are slimmer.

- Pills show On or Off
- Do Not Disturb pill
- Volume and brightness bars: 24 px, with a settings button each
- Calendar: today marked with a pill

## Restore points

**Helpers.** Restore points are easier to use.

You can read and delete restore points without a password, and Free up space can clear older ones.

- Read restore points without a password
- Delete restore points
- Free up space can clear older restore points
- Your apps and Remove an app list web apps and Android apps
- One sign-out notice after an update that changes the desktop

[See Looks after itself](https://shuffleplasma.com/care)

## Notes

**Notes.** Notes reads more plainly and matches Search’s size.

Its words are simpler, the board is clearer, there is always a way out, and it has a new icon.

- Plainer wording
- Opens at Search’s size
- Clearer board
- Always a way out
- New icon

## Smaller fixes

- **Search:** Search holds still when the on-screen keys come up, grows downward, and Esc closes it.
- **Settings:** Applying a Global Theme again keeps your own colours.
- **Settings:** Ctrl + Shift + Esc opens System Monitor on every account, and Meta + Esc still leaves Cards.
- **Helpers:** Updates take effect at the first sign-in after they install.
- **Keyboard:** On a touch screen, Paste comes up with a press and hold instead of a tap.
- **Keyboard:** The on-screen keys put themselves away when Spread opens.
- **Status:** Notification cards line up in a column and no longer overlap.
- **Cards:** Closing a pane refills the screen, and the + button keeps Table open on a New tab.
- **Settings:** Turning on Folder View brings desktop icons back.
