What reached Shuffle in the week starting Monday 16 November 2026.

## Quick actions

**Search.** Search can now do things for you, not only find them.

Type what you want done and it appears as a Quick action above the results. Press Enter, or tap Open, and it happens.

- Quick actions show above Search results
- Reminder: “remind me” or “reminder” + what and when, e.g. “remind me to call the bank tomorrow at 3”
- Reminder with no time is set for 9 AM the next morning
- Tab changes a reminder’s time before you open it
- Note: “note” or “new note” + words, e.g. “note: milk, eggs”
- Do Not Disturb: “dnd” or “do not disturb”, optional time, e.g. “dnd 1h”, “dnd until 5”
- Bluetooth: “bluetooth on” / “bluetooth off”
- Keep awake: “keep awake”, optional time
- Undo button and Ctrl + Z on every switch
- Read on your computer only: no network, no AI model
- New page: Settings › Search and Ask › Quick actions

## Workspaces on the Context Panel

**Status.** The top panel shows your workspaces and the card in view as marks and icons.

Your workspaces lead the Context Panel as numbers or glyphs, followed by the icon of the card you are using, or the icons of its Stack. You choose how much it shows.

- New page: Settings › Context › Workspaces and cards
- Workspaces: Current (default), Pinned or All
- Show each as: Number (default) or Glyph
- Pinned workspaces: a switch for each workspace, shown while Pinned is chosen
- Workspace name: off by default
- The card in view shows as its icon; a Stack shows up to 4 icons, with a pill at either end to page through the rest
- App name after its icon: on by default
- Spread shows its middle card the same way
- Window names in title bars: off by default, turn on in Settings › Workspace › Title bars

## Saved layouts

**Cards.** Saved layouts are drawn as pictures of your screens, and open from Table.

Keep the apps you have open as a layout, see each one as a picture of where its windows stand, and open it into a workspace of its own.

- Settings › Workspace › Layouts › Your layouts: each layout drawn as the screens it was kept from
- What is open now: your screen as it stands; name it to keep it as a layout
- Tap a layout to see it on the screen, then Open
- Table: tap + for Empty workspace and your saved layouts
- A layout opened from Table gets a new workspace named after it; nothing is closed
- A layout keeps where each group stood and which card was in front
- An app already open on another workspace is brought over when its layout opens
- Layouts island: Settings › Actions › Islands › Layouts island

## Apps come to you

**Cards.** Opening an app that is open on another workspace brings it to you.

Each workspace stays a place of its own. Ask for an app from the dock or Search and its window comes to the workspace you are on, instead of taking you away.

- Settings › Workspace › Cards › Apps open on another workspace: Bring here, Go to it, Leave it
- Set up chooses Bring here; Plasma’s own default is Go to it
- A tap on a pinned app in the dock brings its window from another workspace instead of starting it again

## Cards that open from cards

**Cards.** A window opened from a card can land next to that card.

When a link opens the browser, or one app opens another, Shuffle can place the new window with the card it came from instead of on its own.

- New page: Settings › Workspace › Layouts › When one app opens another
- Choices: Opens as today (default), Follows the pairs, Stacks for every app
- Per pair: In front, one Stack · Beside it · On its own
- In front: close the new window and the card it came from is back
- Starter pairs: Mail opens your browser, Files opens a picture, document or video
- Add a pair: choose the app you are in, then the app it opens

## Apps that size themselves

**Cards.** You choose which apps pick their own size.

Some apps, games especially, prefer to open at a size of their own. You can now allow that for each app.

- An app’s menu in the dock: Let it size itself
- Full list: Settings › Workspace › Cards › Apps that size themselves
- An allowed app keeps the size it opens at

## Status on every display

**Status.** Status follows you onto every display.

Each screen gets its own status bar with your notifications, and Keep awake is one switch that can turn itself off at a time you choose.

- Status bar on every display, each with its own notifications
- Keep awake: one switch, optional end time
- Keep awake added to Control Center’s settings
- Do Not Disturb follows Plasma’s timed setting

## Smaller fixes

- **Cards:** Dragging the middle of a Bento layout moves both dividers at once, and a divider stops before a pane gets too small for its title bar.
- **Cards:** A new window appears once it has drawn into its place, instead of flashing first.
- **Notes:** Adding a note from a reminder card starts the note with the reminder already set.
- **Dock:** The thin coloured line that Plasma drew above the dock is gone.
- **Settings:** Small buttons no longer show a stray line or cross under the pointer.
- **Settings:** The sign-in screen shows your wallpaper reliably, even after you change it.
- **Settings:** Panels on several displays are named after the screen they actually sit on.
- **Helpers:** Everything Shuffle downloads is checked before it is used.
- **Helpers:** A Quick actions Helper lists what you can type; tap one and its words go into Search.
- **Helpers:** Layouts kept by an early version open again instead of being skipped.
- **Cards:** A window closing in a layout stays still and dims under the pane growing over it, instead of shrinking toward its middle.
- **Search:** Typing “dnd” or “keep awake” on its own now turns that switch the other way from where it stands.
- **Status:** The “While you were focused” island shows every time Do Not Disturb ends, and its count matches the list it opens.
- **Status:** Opening what Do Not Disturb held shows every notification, not one per app.
- **Status:** The now-playing island shows the right app when that app also runs a second, hidden player.
- **Status:** On a display you are not using, the Context Panel names the card’s app, not its window title.
- **Settings:** The keys sheet lists the workspace keys: Meta + Ctrl + arrows, and Meta or Ctrl + F1 to F9.
- **Settings:** Earlier and Later for the default reminder time are the same small round buttons the sliders use.
- **Settings:** Signing in keeps its glass up until your saved apps start coming back, instead of showing an empty desktop first.
- **Settings:** The sign-in screen starts again by itself if its window manager crashes.
