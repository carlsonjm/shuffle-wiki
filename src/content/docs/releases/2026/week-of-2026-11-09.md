What reached Shuffle in the week starting Monday 9 November 2026.

## One Settings

**Settings.** Settings is now one window with every page in it.

Shuffle’s Settings opens as a real window with a side bar, and every KDE page is inside it, with the everyday ones drawn in Shuffle’s own style.

- Settings opens as a full window with a side bar on the left
- Every KDE settings page is inside it
- Shuffle’s own pages: Wi-Fi and network, Bluetooth, Sound, Screens, Power and battery, Accessibility
- Shuffle’s own pages: Keyboard, mouse and touch, Shortcuts, Apps, Date, Printers, People, Privacy
- Reopens on your last page within 1 hour

## More ways to make it yours

**Settings.** You have far more room to set Shuffle up your way.

Corners, gaps, banners, fonts, title bars and the dock all have wider choices, so Shuffle’s defaults are a starting point rather than a rule.

- Corners: 0 to 30 px
- Space between cards: 0 to 48 px
- Hold before stacking: 100 to 2000 ms
- Banner stays: 1 second to 2 minutes
- Hide title bars: on or off
- Wider ranges for fonts, icons, pointer and dock height
- Active window outline: Soft, Accent or Off
- Your own wallpaper picture sets its own two colours

[See Make it yours](https://shuffleplasma.com/yours)

## Quiet while sharing

**Status.** Notifications stay quiet while you share your screen.

Banners hold back during a screen share, and afterwards a short summary tells you what arrived while you were sharing.

- Banners hold while your screen is shared
- The shared screen’s island says notifications are quiet
- Summary afterwards: While you were sharing
- On from Set up; switch in Settings › Notifications › Quiet while sharing

## Panels your way

**Settings.** Panels can stay on screen, come with Spread, or hide until you need them.

Each display can have its own choice, and both panels now live together on one Panels page in Settings.

- Panels show: Always, With Spread, or Hide automatically
- Set per display
- Both panels on one page: Settings › Panels
- Context Bar shows a Stack’s tabs and opens the Stack sheet

## Type in Spread

**Search.** Typing in Spread opens Search with what you typed.

Start typing while your cards are spread out and Search picks up the words, with no need to open it first.

- Start typing in Spread: Search opens with your words

## Keep awake

**Status.** Keep awake arrived in Control Center.

One tap keeps the screen from going to sleep, for a presentation or a long download.

- Keep awake pill in Control Center

## Smaller fixes

- **Cards:** Connected Cards move more smoothly, title bars grab where you expect, and Stacks keep their size when restored.
- **Cards:** Save and open dialogs float over your work instead of becoming a card.
- **Cards:** You can step through a Stack one card at a time, take its front card out, and snap the front card to an edge without the Stack falling apart.
- **Keyboard:** The on-screen keys stay away while you type on a real keyboard, and rest while an app waits for a shortcut.
- **Status:** Notifications follow each app’s own rules, and play its sound where you allow it.
- **Search:** A result’s highlight stays on its own row, and two Search widgets on one panel no longer repeat an island.
- **Settings:** Signing out is smoother, and the sign-in screen follows your desktop when you change its look.
