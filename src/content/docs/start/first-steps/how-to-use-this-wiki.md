This wiki explains how to use Shuffle, one task per page. Each page starts with the short answer in grey under its title, so you can often stop reading there.

## Find a page

- **Search.** Type what you want into the search box at the top of every page, such as **printer** or **turn off cards**, and choose a result. Search finds words a page uses and the everyday words people use for the same thing, so **hotkeys** finds [Every keyboard shortcut](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/shortcuts/).
- **Areas.** The tabs along the top split the wiki into areas: **Get started**, **Shuffle**, **Apps and devices**, **Releases** and **Open source**. Choose one to see its own list on the left.
- **Groups.** In the list on the left, each area is split into groups, such as **Cards and workspaces** or **Notes**. Tap or click a group's name to open it and see its pages.
- **On this page.** Longer pages list their sections at the right, or under **On this page** at the top on a phone or tablet. Choose one to jump to it.

On a narrow screen, the list on the left folds into the menu button at the top.

## How the pages are written

- **Keys** look like this: <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>. Press them together. **Meta** is the key with the Windows logo on most keyboards, and every Meta on this wiki links to [Keys and the Meta key](/start/first-steps/meta-key/).
- **Touch and the mouse** are given together where they work the same way, as in "tap or click". Where they differ, the page gives each one.
- **Names in bold**, such as **Settings** or **Free up space**, are what you see on the screen. Type them into Search to open them.
- **Grey boxes** hold a line to paste into Konsole, the app for typing commands. [Paste a line into Konsole](/apps/devices-and-files/konsole/) shows how. Each box holds one line. Paste it, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, and wait for it to finish before the next one.
- **Coloured boxes** hold a note or a warning worth reading before you start.

## When something is wrong

Start at [Something is not right](/shuffle/when-something-goes-wrong/something-is-not-right/). It lists problems by what you see, and points to the page that fixes each one.

## Keep up with changes

Shuffle updates every week. [Releases](/releases/) lists what changed in each week since launch. Every page here says at its foot when it last changed.

## Something missing or wrong here

Every page has **Edit page** at its foot, which opens it on GitHub. If you have a GitHub account, you can suggest a change there.
