Once Shuffle is [installed](/start/first-steps/install/), the **Set up Shuffle** window opens. It shows you around, asks a few short questions, and then asks you to sign out once. The next time you sign in, Shuffle is on and the tour opens.

## What Set up asks

Each step has a picture of your Shuffle that changes as you choose. You can skip any step and change it later in **Settings**.

- **Your look:** light or dark, a highlight colour, and a wallpaper.
- **The buttons on the Actions Panel:** Spread, Table and the keyboard.
- **Search:** what it shows before you type, and the key that opens it.
- **The Context Panel:** the clock, the weather and the islands.
- **An app store,** on a computer that has none. It adds Discover only if you press **Add and finish**.
- **Shuffle picks:** apps Shuffle recommends. They are all switched off to start, and nothing is installed unless you switch it on.

## What Set up changes for you

- **The Actions Panel** takes over the apps pinned to your old bottom panel, and adds **Notes** and **Settings**.
- **The wallpaper** becomes one of Shuffle's if yours was the one the computer came with. A picture you chose stays.
- **The desktop's icons** are hidden. Your files stay in the **Desktop** folder, which you can open in [Files](/shuffle/search/files/).
- **Keys:** <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> opens [Search](/shuffle/search/search/), <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd> opens Files, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd> opens System Monitor, unless you had given those keys to something else.
- **The on-screen keys** come up when you tap a text field on a touchscreen.

## Try these first

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> and type the name of an app. Tap or click it to open it. It opens as a [card](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/cards/).
2. Open a second app. Then swipe up from the bottom edge, or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>, to see both in [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/).
3. Drag a window by its title bar to the left edge of the screen to [put two apps side by side](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/side-by-side/).
4. Tap the network icon at the top right to open [Control Center](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/) for Wi-Fi, sound and brightness.
5. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Check for updates**, and open it. [Helpers](/shuffle/helpers/helpers/) like this one look after the computer.
6. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>/</kbd> to see [every keyboard shortcut](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/shortcuts/).

## See the tour again

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open **Settings**, then choose **About**. **Take the tour** shows eight short pages on where things are. **More tips** has step-by-step lessons on the Actions Panel, Search, the keys and more.

## Change your mind

Everything Set up chose is in **Settings**. See [Find your way around Settings](/shuffle/settings/settings/). To go back to plain Plasma, see [Turn Shuffle off or remove it](/shuffle/when-something-goes-wrong/turn-off/).
