## Get around with the touchpad

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| See your cards in [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/) | Swipe four fingers down on the touchpad, or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>. |
| See your workspaces in [Table](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/table/) | Swipe four fingers up, or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd>. |
| Open [Search](/shuffle/search/search/) | Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>. |
| Open Shuffle Settings | Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd>. |

See [Every keyboard shortcut](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/shortcuts/) for the rest.

## The battery

The battery and how much charge is left sit at the right end of the Context Panel. Tap or click it for the **Power and Battery** page.

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Power and battery**:

- **Power mode** chooses **Quiet**, **Balanced** or **Performance**.
- **Settings for** has its own set of choices for **Plugged in**, on battery and **Low battery**: when to **Dim the screen**, **Turn the screen off**, what happens **When you step away**, and the brightness.
- **Battery care** has **Limit charging**, which keeps the battery healthy when the laptop is plugged in most of the time. **Stop charging at** sets where it stops.
- **Battery health** shows how much the battery holds, compared with new.

On laptops that support it, Control Center also has the [performance profile](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/), with its own charge limit.

## Closing the lid

Under **Lid and button**, **Closing the lid** chooses what happens, such as **Sleep** or **Lock**. **Even with a monitor plugged in** chooses whether that holds while a monitor is plugged in. Off, closing the lid only turns off the laptop's own screen, and you carry on on the monitor.

**The power button** chooses what pressing it does.
