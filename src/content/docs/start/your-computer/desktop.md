## Arrange your displays

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Screens**.

1. Drag each display in the picture to where it sits on your desk. **Identify displays** shows which is which.
2. **Which display comes first** chooses where new windows open and where the panels sit.
3. Choose a display to tune it: **Resolution**, **Size of everything**, **Refresh rate**, **Adaptive sync**, **HDR** and **Brightness**.

After a change, Shuffle asks whether to keep it. If you cannot see the screen, it goes back by itself after a few seconds.

## The panels on monitors

On a monitor, both panels are always on screen. **Context Panel on**, in **Panels**, chooses whether every screen has its own top bar, with its own clock, or only the main screen. Notifications show on the main screen. See [Hide the panels or keep them showing](/shuffle/settings/panels/).

## Cards on several displays

Each display has its own Spread and its own cards. To move a card to another display, see [Use cards on more than one display](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/several-displays/). Cards from a display you unplug go to the **Primary display**, which you choose in **Workspace**, then **Spread**. With no touchscreen, the largest display is primary.

## A big monitor

On a big display, cards you open sit side by side instead of filling the screen. You can also divide a monitor into your own [zones](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/zones/). See [Cards on a big display](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/big-displays/).
