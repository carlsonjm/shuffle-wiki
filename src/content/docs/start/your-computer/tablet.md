Shuffle is built for touch first. On a tablet, or a laptop that folds or detaches into one, everything works with your fingers.

## Get around with your fingers

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| See your cards in [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/) | Swipe up from the bottom edge, or put three fingers on the screen and swipe down. |
| See your workspaces in [Table](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/table/) | Pull down from the top edge, or swipe three fingers up. |
| Open [Search](/shuffle/search/search/) | Tap the dot at the left end of the Actions Panel. |
| Close an app | In Spread, flick its card up. |
| Open an app's sheet | Touch and hold its icon on the Actions Panel. |

A swipe in from the left or right edge can do whatever you choose. See [Touch and touchpad gestures](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/touch/).

## The panels on a tablet

On the tablet's own screen, the Actions Panel along the bottom comes with Spread and Table, so your apps have the whole screen. Tap the bottom edge to bring it for a moment. The Context Panel along the top stays on screen, as a phone keeps the time in sight. To change either, see [Hide the panels or keep them showing](/shuffle/settings/panels/).

## Type

With no keyboard attached, tap a text field and the [on-screen keys](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/on-screen-keyboard/) come up. The keyboard pearl on the Actions Panel raises and lowers them too. Hold the space bar to [move the cursor](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/keyboard-trackpad/).

**Tablet mode**, in **Keyboard, mouse and touch** in Shuffle Settings, makes touch targets bigger and brings the keys up. **When needed** turns it on when the keyboard is folded away or detached. See [Change the keyboard](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/keyboard-settings/).

## Turn the screen

In Shuffle Settings, choose **Screens**. **Turn by itself** turns the screen as you turn the tablet, **In tablet mode** or always. **Turn** sets it by hand.

## One card at a time

On a small screen, a card you open fills the screen, and Spread shows one card at a time. To keep cards side by side instead, open Shuffle Settings, choose **Workspace**, then **Spread**, and set **The screen shows** to **Side by side**.

## With a monitor plugged in

The touchscreen is where Spread lives, and [Table](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/table/) opens on the touchscreen when you call it with a finger. See [Use Shuffle with monitors](/start/your-computer/desktop/).

## Battery

- [Keep awake](/shuffle/around-the-screen/keep-awake/) stops the screen from dimming while you read or watch.
- **Power and battery**, in Shuffle Settings, sets when the screen dims and the tablet sleeps. See [Use Shuffle on a laptop](/start/your-computer/laptop/).
