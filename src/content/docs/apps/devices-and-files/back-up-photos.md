A backup is a copy somewhere else. A copy on this computer's own drive isn't a backup.

1. Plug in a USB drive.
2. Open **Files**: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>. The USB drive shows on the left. Not there? See [A USB drive doesn't show up](/apps/devices-and-files/usb-drive/).
3. Right-click the **Pictures** folder and choose **Copy**.
4. Click the USB drive on the left, right-click an empty space, and choose **Paste here**.

:::note
Or upload the Pictures folder to a cloud service, such as Google Photos or OneDrive, in your web browser.
:::
