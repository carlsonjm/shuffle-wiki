1. Press the <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> key, or tap the dot at the left end of the dock along the bottom of the screen. Search opens.
2. Type **Free up space** and tap it.
3. It shows what fills your disk, and clears old copies and leftovers when you say so.

:::note
Big apps you don't use? See [Remove an app](/apps/getting-apps/remove-app/).
:::
