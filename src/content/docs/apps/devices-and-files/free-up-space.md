## Free up space

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, type **Free up space**, and tap it.
2. It lists what it can clear, each with how much room it takes:
   - **Old versions of installed apps.** The version you use now always stays.
   - **Parts nothing uses**, and **App parts nothing uses**, left by apps that are gone.
   - **System logs**, records of what the computer did. The most recent stay.
   - **Crash reports**, only needed to report a crash.
   - **Old cache files**, spare copies apps have not used for a month. Apps make them again when needed.
   - **Community apps' build files.**
   - What is in the **Trash**.
   - **Older restore points.**
3. **Show which** names exactly what a row would remove.
4. Tap **Free up space**.

It takes a [restore point](/shuffle/helpers/restore-points/) first, and writes in your notebook what it removed and how to get it back. See [What changed](/shuffle/helpers/undo-a-change/).

## Tidy up Downloads

The last row of Free up space opens **Tidy up Downloads**. You can also open it from Search by its name. It gathers the files in your **Downloads** folder and on the **Desktop** into groups:

- Installers of apps you already have, and Windows programs. These two are ticked for you.
- Copies the same as their original.
- Files you have not opened in 30 days.
- Screenshots.
- The folders there, each with its size.

Tick or untick what you like, then confirm. The ticked files go to the **Trash**, where you can still put them back. Empty the Trash to free the space for good.

:::note
Big apps you don't use? See [Remove an app](/apps/getting-apps/remove-app/), or open **Your apps** from Search, which lists the apps you added, biggest first.
:::
