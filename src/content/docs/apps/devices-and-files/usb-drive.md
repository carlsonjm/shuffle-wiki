Right after a system update, a newly plugged-in drive may not show. Restarting finishes the update, and then the drive shows.

1. Unplug the drive.
2. Restart the computer: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Del</kbd>, then choose **Restart**.
3. Plug the drive in again. It shows on the left in **Files** (<kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>).
