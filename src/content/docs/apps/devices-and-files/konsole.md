Some answers here give you a line to paste into Konsole, the app where you type commands. You don't need to understand the line; copying it exactly is enough.

1. Click **Copy** beside the line on this page.
2. Open Konsole. Press the <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> key, or tap the dot at the left end of the dock along the bottom of the screen. Search opens. Type **Konsole** and tap it.
3. Paste with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd>. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> on its own doesn't work in Konsole. Or right-click and choose **Paste**.
4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
5. If it asks for your password, type the one you sign in with and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Nothing shows while you type; that's normal.
6. If it asks a yes-or-no question, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to accept.
