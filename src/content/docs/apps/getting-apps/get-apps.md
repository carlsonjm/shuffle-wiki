Apps come from Discover, the app store. It works like the app store on a phone.

1. Open **Discover**, the app store: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Discover** and tap it. Not there at all? See [Add the app store](/apps/getting-apps/add-app-store/).
2. Click the search box at the top left and type the app's name, such as **Steam** or **Spotify**.
3. Click the app in the results, then **Install**. Type your password if asked.
4. When it finishes, the button says **Launch**. Next time, open the app from Search. Press the <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> key, or tap the dot at the left end of the dock along the bottom of the screen. Search opens. Type its name.

:::note
Most apps in Discover come from Flathub. They update through Discover, too.
:::
