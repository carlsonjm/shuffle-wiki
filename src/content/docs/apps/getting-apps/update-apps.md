---
title: "Keep the computer up to date"
description: "Check for updates, in Search, updates everything at once."
keywords: "update upgrade new version apps system pacman"
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

1. Press the <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> key, or tap the dot at the left end of the dock along the bottom of the screen. Search opens.
2. Type **Check for updates** and tap it.
3. Follow what it shows. It updates the system, your apps and Shuffle together.

Or paste this line into Konsole (see [Paste a line into Konsole](/apps/devices-and-files/konsole/)), then restart the computer.

```sh frame="terminal" title="Konsole"
sudo pacman -Syu
```
