---
title: "Remove an app"
description: "In Discover, choose Installed, then Remove."
keywords: "remove uninstall delete app program get rid add or remove programs"
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

1. Open **Discover**, the app store: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Discover** and tap it. Not there at all? See [Add the app store](/apps/getting-apps/add-app-store/).
2. Click **Installed** on the left.
3. Find the app, click it, then **Remove**.

:::note
Removing an app leaves your files where they are.
:::
