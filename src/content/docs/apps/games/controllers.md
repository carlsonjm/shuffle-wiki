---

1. With a cable: plug it in. That's all.
2. Wireless: put the controller in pairing mode (see its instructions). Open **System Settings** from Search, click **Bluetooth**, then **Pair a New Device**, and pick the controller.
3. Open your game. Steam sets up most controllers by itself.
