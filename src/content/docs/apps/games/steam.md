1. Open **Discover**, the app store: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Discover** and tap it. Not there at all? See [Add the app store](/apps/getting-apps/add-app-store/).
2. Click the search box at the top left and type **Steam**.
3. Click **Steam** in the results, then **Install**. Type your password if asked. It's a big download.
4. Click **Launch**. The first time, Steam updates itself for a minute or two; let it finish.
5. Sign in with your Steam account. Your games are in your library, ready to install.

:::note
Most Windows games on Steam play here too. If one won't start, open its **Properties** in Steam, then **Compatibility**, and tick **Force the use of a specific Steam Play compatibility tool**.
:::
