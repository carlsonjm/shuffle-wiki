Epic and GOG don't make an app for Linux. Heroic Games Launcher is a free app that signs in to both and plays your games.

1. Open **Discover**, the app store: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Discover** and tap it. Not there at all? See [Add the app store](/apps/getting-apps/add-app-store/).
2. Search for **Heroic Games Launcher**, click it, then **Install**.
3. Click **Launch**, and sign in to Epic, GOG or both. Your library is there.

:::note
Most games play. A few with strict anti-cheat, such as Fortnite, don't run on Linux at all.
:::
