---

1. Open your web browser: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Firefox** and tap it.
2. Go to **office.com** and sign in with your Microsoft account. Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneDrive all work there.
