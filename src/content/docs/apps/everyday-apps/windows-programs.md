Windows programs, the .exe files, don't run here. Most popular apps have a Linux version, and many work in the web browser.

1. Open **Discover**, the app store: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Discover** and tap it. Not there at all? See [Add the app store](/apps/getting-apps/add-app-store/).
2. Search for the app's name. If it's there, click it, then **Install**.
3. Not there? Search for the job instead, such as **photo editor** or **video editor**, and pick one of the results.
