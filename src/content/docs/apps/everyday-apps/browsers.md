Your computer came with a web browser, usually Firefox. To open it, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Firefox** and tap it.

1. Open **Discover**, the app store: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Discover** and tap it. Not there at all? See [Add the app store](/apps/getting-apps/add-app-store/).
2. Search for **Google Chrome**, **Brave** or **Microsoft Edge**.
3. Click it, then **Install**.
