1. Open **Files**: press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>.
2. Find the PDF, often in **Downloads** on the left, and double-click it. It opens in the PDF reader that came with your computer.
