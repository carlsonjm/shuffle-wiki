## Copy, cut and paste

1. [Choose the files](/shuffle/files/choose-files/).
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd> to copy them, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>X</kbd> to cut them. **Copy** and **Cut** are in the row above the files too.
3. Open the folder where they should go.
4. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd>, or tap or click **Paste**. A copy lands there, or what you cut moves there.

To paste straight into a folder without opening it, right-click the folder and choose **Paste into it**.

## Drag them

Drag the files with the mouse, or touch and hold them and then drag. A label says where they would land. Let go on a folder, a place on the left or a drive, and they are copied there. Let go on **Trash** and they go to the Trash.

Near the top or bottom of the list, it scrolls as you drag.

## When a name is already taken

If a file of the same name is already there, Files asks what to do: **Replace**, **Skip**, **Keep both** or **Stop**. For a folder, **Merge** takes the place of **Replace**. When several files arrive at once, tick **Do the same for the rest** to answer once for all of them. Nothing is replaced unless you choose **Replace**.

## Copy to and from other apps

- **To another app:** drag the files past the edge of Files and drop them on the app. It gets a copy, and the originals stay where they were.
- **From another app:** drag files from an app on another display over Files. They are copied into the folder under them, never moved.

## While a copy is running

A long copy or move shows its progress, with **Pause**, **Resume** and **×** to stop it. You can keep using Files meanwhile.
