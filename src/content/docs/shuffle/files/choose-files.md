Choose files first, then act on them with the row above the files, the action menu, or the keys.

## With a mouse

- **One file:** click it.
- **Add or take away one:** hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and click it.
- **A run of files:** click the first, then hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and click the last.
- **A box of files:** drag across empty space. Everything the box touches is chosen. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to add to what you had, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> to flip each one.
- **None:** click empty space.

Double-click a file to open it in its usual app.

## With touch

- **Start choosing several:** touch and hold a file. A circle appears on every file.
- **Add or take away one:** tap it while the circles show.
- **Stop:** tap **Done**.

A single tap on a file opens it.

## With the keyboard

| Press | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The arrow keys, <kbd>Home</kbd> or <kbd>End</kbd> | Moves to the next file, the next row, or the first or last file. |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> with those keys | Makes the choice bigger. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> | Chooses everything in the folder. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Opens the chosen file. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Clears the choice. |

## What you can do with them

While files are chosen, the row above them offers **Copy**, **Cut**, **Rename** (for one file) and **Move to Trash**. Right-click a file, or tap **⋯**, for everything else: **Open with**, **Compress**, **Hide**, **Properties** and more. See [Rename, compress and organise](/shuffle/files/organise/).
