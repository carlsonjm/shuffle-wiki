Right-click a file, or [choose it](/shuffle/files/choose-files/) and tap **⋯**, to open its action menu.

## Rename

Choose one file and press <kbd>F2</kbd>, or choose **Rename**. Its name is ready to type, with the ending such as **.pdf** left as it was. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to keep it, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave it.

## Make a folder

Tap or click **New folder** in the row above the files. Type a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Open with another app

Choose **Open with**. The usual app comes first, and **Show all applications** lists every one. Tick **Always use it** to make your choice the usual app for that kind of file from now on.

## Compress and extract

- **Compress:** makes a .zip file next to the first chosen file. One file keeps its name; several make **Archive.zip**.
- **Extract here:** on a .zip or other archive, unpacks it next to itself. If it holds several things, they go into a folder of their own.

Progress shows on an island on the Actions Panel.

## Hide and unhide

Choose **Hide** to take files out of sight without deleting them. To see them again, tap the eye inside the search field at the top. Hidden files show dimmed. Choose one and pick **Unhide** to bring it back for good. Files and folders whose names start with a dot are always hidden this way.

## See a file's details

Choose one file or folder and press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or choose **Properties**. It shows the kind, size, folder and dates. A folder shows its total size once counted. A photo adds its size in pixels, the date taken and the camera, a song its title, artist and length, and a video its size and length.
