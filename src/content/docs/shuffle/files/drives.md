When you plug in a drive, it appears under the places on the left of Files, with how much room it has left. An island on the Actions Panel shows it too.

- **Open it:** tap or click it. It says **Opening…** for a moment.
- **Eject it:** tap or click **⏏** beside it. It says **Ejecting…**, then Files says you can unplug it. If Files is still copying to or from it, it says **Ejects when done**, and ejects once the copy finishes.

A plugged-in phone opens in Files too. If a drive does not show up at all, see [A USB drive doesn't show up](/apps/devices-and-files/usb-drive/).
