## Move files to the Trash

[Choose the files](/shuffle/files/choose-files/) and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or tap or click **Move to Trash**. Files asks first, then moves them. Nothing is deleted for good this way.

## Get them back

- **The last one:** right-click empty space, or touch and hold it, and choose **Restore last trashed item**. Choose it again for the one before.
- **Any of them:** tap or click **Trash** among the places on the left. Each file shows the folder it came from. Choose the ones you want and tap **Restore**. They go back where they were.

## Delete for good

In the Trash, choose files and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or tap **Delete for good**. Files asks first. This cannot be undone.

To empty the whole Trash, right-click empty space and choose **Empty Trash**, or touch and hold **Trash** among the places. Files says how many items and how much space first.
