## Places

The places on the left are your usual folders, such as **Home**, **Documents** and **Downloads**, then your drives.

- **Add one:** tap or click **+** beside **Fold**, then **Add this folder** or **Add a folder…**. Or drag a folder into the empty room under the places.
- **Rename, hide or remove one:** touch and hold it, or right-click it.
- **Reorder them:** drag a place up or down with the mouse.
- **Show or hide them:** tap **+**, then **Show or hide places**.
- **Make them wider or narrower:** drag their edge. Tap **Fold** to shrink them to a row of icons.

Places are shared with Dolphin and with every app's Open and Save windows.

## Tabs

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Open a tab | Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>T</kbd>, or tap **+** in the tab row. To open a folder in its own tab, right-click it and choose **Open in new tab**. |
| Go to the next tab | Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>, or tap the tab. |
| Close a tab | Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd>, or tap its **×**. |

The tab row only shows while two or more tabs are open.

## Sort, size and hidden files

- **Sort:** tap the pill after **New folder**, then choose name A–Z, name Z–A, newest first or largest first.
- **Size:** tap **−** or **+**, pinch, or hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and turn the mouse wheel. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>0</kbd> returns to the usual size.
- **Hidden files:** tap the eye inside the search field.

## Bigger, or a window of its own

- **Expand**, at the top right, makes Files fill the screen. Tap it again to go back to Search's size.
- **Pop out**, beside it, opens Files in a window of its own, with your tabs as they were. Each Pop out opens another window. Once Files has a window, <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd> brings that window forward.
