Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Appearance**.

- **Wallpaper:** choose a picture, or add your own. **The desktop shows** switches between a picture, a slideshow and a plain color.
- **Colors:** **Theme: dark or light** sets the theme. **Follow the sun** turns dark at sunset and light at sunrise. **Highlight** sets the color that marks what is chosen.
- **Fonts:** **Font for everything** sets the typeface, and **Font for code and terminals** the one Konsole uses.
- **Shape and motion:** how rounded things are, and **Motion speed** for how fast things move.

To make everything larger, open **Screens** in Shuffle Settings and change **Size of everything**. **Accessibility** has **Text size** too.
