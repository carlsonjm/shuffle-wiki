Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Appearance**. It has five parts: **Wallpaper**, **Colors**, **Icons and pointer**, **Fonts**, and **Shape and motion**.

## Pick a look

A **look** is a wallpaper with its two colors. Under **Wallpaper**, tap one of **Shuffle's looks** to use it. **Surprise me** picks one for you.

To use a picture of your own, tap the tile that adds one, and choose the picture. Shuffle takes two colors from it. **The look's colors** keeps the colors you had instead, and **From my picture** goes back to the picture's.

## The wallpaper

- **The desktop shows** chooses a **Picture**, a **Slideshow** of pictures from your folders, or a **Plain color**.
- **The picture** chooses how it fits: it **Fills the screen**, **Fits inside it**, **Stretches to it**, **Sits in the center** or **Repeats as tiles**. **Around the picture** fills the space left with **The picture, blurred** or **A color**.
- For a slideshow, **Add a folder of pictures**, then choose **A new picture every** and **In this order**.
- With more than one screen, **Display** chooses whether the wallpaper goes on **Every display** or just one.

## Colors

- **Theme** chooses **Dark**, **Light**, or **Follow the sun**, which is light from sunrise and dark from sunset.
- **Primary** tints the backgrounds, and **How much color** sets how strongly. Text stays easy to read.
- **Highlight** is the color that marks what is chosen.
- **Color windows** gives apps' pages and title bars the primary color, Konsole and Kate too. **Color Shuffle** does the same for Search, Files, Status' pop-ups and Settings.

**Active window outline** draws a line around the window you type into while others are beside it: **Soft**, **Accent** or **Off**.

Open windows follow in a few seconds.

## Icons and pointer

- **Icons** chooses the icons apps and folders wear. **Get more icons** finds more from KDE's store.
- **Pointer** chooses **Ink**, dark with a white edge, or **Light**, white with a dark edge, or **Another pointer**. **Pointer size** sets its size.

## Fonts

- **Font for everything** is used by apps, menus, title bars and Settings.
- **Font for code and terminals** is the one Konsole uses, with every letter the same width.
- **Text size** makes all text larger or smaller.
- **Choose each font** sets a font, size and weight for main text, small text, toolbars, menus, title bars and code separately.
- **Letter edges** has **Smooth the letters**, **Sharpen the letters** and **Color at the edges**, for a screen where letters look rough or show colored fringes.

## Shape and motion

- **Shape** moves between **Tight** and **Airy**, and between **crisp** and **soft** corners. **Energy** sets how lively things feel.
- **Motion speed** sets how fast things move.
- **Back to Shuffle's** puts both back as they came.

To make everything on screen larger, not just the text, open **Screens** in Shuffle Settings and change **Size of everything**.
