Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Workspace**. It has five parts: **Title bars**, **Cards**, **Stacks**, **Spread** and **Layouts**.

## Title bars

- **Hide title bars** takes the bar, name and buttons off every window, and gives the room to the app. Close a window then with the close gesture, from its icon on the Actions Panel, or with <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>F4</kbd>. Some apps draw a bar of their own and keep it.
- **Window names in title bars** shows each window's name. Off, the Context Panel names the app in use, and each title bar keeps only its buttons.
- **Buttons sit** on the **Right** or the **Left**. **Buttons shown** chooses **Close and size** or **All three**. **Your own order** lets you place each button, **Keep on top** too.
- **Height** sets how tall the title bars are.

## Cards

- **Space between** sets the gap between cards and around them. Below 10 px, swipes from the screen's edge may not always register.
- **Corners** and **Shadows** set how round the cards are and how deep their shadows go.
- **Arrange windows** on each monitor **Automatically** or **In my zones**. See [zones](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/zones/).
- **Apps open on another workspace** chooses what happens when you open an app that is already open elsewhere: **Bring here**, **Go to it**, or **Leave it**.
- **Apps that size themselves** lists apps that keep their own size, such as games. Tap one to forget the choice.

## Stacks

- **Hold before stacking** sets how long you hold one card on another before they become a [Stack](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/stacks/).
- **Behind a stack in Spread** shows a hairline of the cards behind, or **Nothing**.

## Spread

- **Primary display** chooses where cards from a display you unplug go. Each display has its own Spread.
- **The screen shows** chooses **One card at a time** or **Side by side**, or the automatic choice: one at a time on a small screen, side by side on a big one.
- **Neighbors show** sets how much of the cards beside the Active one you see, from **A sliver** to **The most**.
- **Sleeping cards in Spread** shows cards that are asleep **Dimmed** or at **Full strength**.

## Layouts

See [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/).

The line around the window you type into, **Active window outline**, is in **Appearance**, under **Colors**. To turn the Workspace off, see [About](/shuffle/settings/about/).
