Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Panels**. Under it, **Context** is the panel along the top, and **Actions** is the panel along the bottom.

## The top panel

Choose **Context**:

- **Workspaces and cards** chooses which workspaces the left end shows: the **Current** one, the **Pinned** ones, or **All**. **Show each as** chooses a **Number**, a **Glyph** or **Nothing**, and **Workspace glyphs** picks each one's mark. **Workspace name** and **App name** show the current workspace's name and the name of the app in use. **Pinned workspaces** chooses which ones stay on the bar.
- **Clock** sets the clock and the calendars. See [the calendar and the clock](/shuffle/around-the-screen/calendar-and-weather/).
- **Control Center** sets its order, the power row, and where each icon shows. See [Control Center](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/) and [the system tray](/shuffle/around-the-screen/system-tray/).
- **Weather** sets the weather. See [the weather](/shuffle/around-the-screen/calendar-and-weather/).

## The bottom panel

Choose **Actions**:

- **Pearls** turns the pearls at the start of the panel on or off: **Spread**, **Table** and **Keyboard**. **Search**, the dot, is always there.
- **Size and icons** sets the **Panel height** and the **Icon size**, and **Running apps** chooses whether a dot and line show under an app that is open.
- **Apps and folders** lists your **Pinned apps**. Drag a handle to change the order, or **Unpin** one. **Add apps** pins more. Under **Folders**, **New folder** keeps two or more apps together, and **Rename** and **Ungroup** change one. **Folders open as** chooses **A column of apps** or **A note**.
- **Islands** chooses which [islands](/shuffle/around-the-screen/islands/) show.

You can also pin, reorder and group apps on the panel itself. See [the Actions Panel](/shuffle/around-the-screen/actions-panel/).
