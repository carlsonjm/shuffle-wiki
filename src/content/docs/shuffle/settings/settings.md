## Open Shuffle Settings

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd>. Press it again while Settings is in front to close it. It opens again where you left it.

Its window is called **Settings**, because it holds all of the computer's settings under one roof. This wiki also calls it **Shuffle Settings**. Both mean the same window.

You can also press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Shuffle Settings**, and tap it.

## Find a setting

Type in **Search settings** at the top. Settings lists every match, and a tap goes straight to it. <kbd>Esc</kbd> steps back out of a page.

## Put a change back

The first page of Settings lists **Recently changed**: each change you made in Settings. Tap the button beside one to put it back as it was. A change made by choosing a look or a wallpaper can be put back the same way.

## What is where

**Your Shuffle** holds how Shuffle itself looks and works:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Wallpaper, colors, icons and pointer, fonts, shape and motion. See [Change the look](/shuffle/settings/look/). |
| **Panels** | When the panels along the top and bottom are on screen. See [Hide the panels](/shuffle/settings/panels/). Under it, **Context** holds the top panel's workspaces, clock, Control Center and weather, and **Actions** the bottom panel's pearls, icons, apps, folders and islands. See [Change what the panels hold](/shuffle/settings/panel-contents/). |
| **Workspace** | Title bars, cards, Stacks, Spread and Layouts. See [Change title bars, cards and Spread](/shuffle/settings/workspace/). |
| **Notifications** | What pops up, how long it stays, and what each app may do. See [Notifications](/shuffle/around-the-screen/notifications/). |
| **Search and Ask** | What Search shows, quick actions, and Ask. See [Change how Search looks and opens](/shuffle/search/search-settings/). |
| **Helpers** | What the Helpers tell you about. See [When Helpers speak up](/shuffle/helpers/when-helpers-speak-up/). |

**This Computer** holds the computer's own settings: **Wi-Fi and network**, **Bluetooth**, **Sound**, **Screens**, **Power and battery**, **Accessibility**, **Keyboard, mouse and touch**, **Shortcuts**, **Devices**, **Apps and files**, **People and accounts**, **Date, region and language** and **Privacy and security**.

At the end, **Expert** opens KDE's own settings for anything Shuffle doesn't show, such as **Window rules** and **Desktop effects**. **Open KDE System Settings**, under **Repair**, opens all of Plasma's own settings, for repairs. **About** has the version, the tour, a problem report and **Turn off**. See [About this Shuffle](/shuffle/settings/about/).
