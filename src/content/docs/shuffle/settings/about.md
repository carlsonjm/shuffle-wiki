Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **About**.

## What is installed

The top shows Shuffle's version, and under **Installed** the version of each part.

## The tour and tips

- **Take the tour** opens eight short pages on where things are in Shuffle.
- **More tips** opens step-by-step lessons on the Actions Panel, Search, the keys and more.

## Save a problem report

Under **Help**, tap **Save report**. Shuffle writes a file to your **Downloads** folder with the versions of everything, how Shuffle is set up, and this session's system messages. Those messages can name apps and files you used. Nothing is sent anywhere. **Show** opens the folder.

## Fix the Workspace after a system update

If Spread and Bento stop working after a system update, tap **Fix** beside **Fix the Workspace after a system update**, under **Troubleshooting**. See also [Cards are off after an update](/shuffle/when-something-goes-wrong/cards-off-after-update/).

## Turn the Workspace off

**Shuffle Workspace** has **Turn off**. Windows then open and move the way Plasma does on its own, and Spread, Bento and the Workspace's swipes stop until you turn it back on here. See [Turn Cards off](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/plasma-desktop/).

## Turn Shuffle off

**Turn off** puts your panels, keyboard, look and lock screen back as they were before Shuffle. It asks first. Sign out and back in to finish. Shuffle stays installed, and **Set up** turns it on again. See [Turn Shuffle off or remove it](/shuffle/when-something-goes-wrong/turn-off/).
