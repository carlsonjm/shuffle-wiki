Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Panels**.

## Choose when each panel shows

There is a choice for the **Context Panel, along the top**, and one for the **Actions Panel, along the bottom**. Each has one setting for the computer's own screen, named **Tablet screen**, **Laptop screen** or **Built-in screen**, and one for **Monitors**.

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Always** | The panel is always on screen, and apps stop at its edge. |
| **With Spread** | The panel comes with Spread and Table, over your apps. A tap or the pointer at its edge brings it for a moment. |
| **Hide automatically** | The panel stays out of the way until a tap or the pointer at its edge brings it. |

When a panel is out of view, tap its edge of the screen, or rest the pointer there, and it comes over your cards. A tap brings it for a few seconds, and each touch on it starts them again. The pointer keeps it while it rests on it. Pulling down from the top edge still opens [Table](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/table/).

A panel that is not always on screen leaves the whole height to your apps, so nothing moves under you when it comes and goes.

## For both panels

- **Context Panel on** chooses **Every screen**, where each screen has its own top bar with its own clock, or **Main screen only**. Notifications show on the main screen either way. Change which screen is main in **Screens**.
- **Edge look** chooses how a panel's edge meets your apps: **Fade**, always the soft fade; **Full**, always solid black; or **Automatic**, the soft fade until an app reaches the panel, then solid black.
