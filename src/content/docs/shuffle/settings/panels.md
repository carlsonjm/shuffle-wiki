1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Panels**.
2. Under **Context Panel on screen** and **Actions Panel on screen**, choose when each one shows. You can choose separately for the built-in screen and for monitors.

When a panel is set to stay out of view, tap its edge of the screen, or rest the pointer there, and it comes back over your cards. A tap brings it for a few seconds; the pointer keeps it while it rests on it.
