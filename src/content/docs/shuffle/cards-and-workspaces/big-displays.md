On a big display, Shuffle sets cards **side by side** instead of one at a time. Each card takes a **pane**, a part of the screen, and the panes share the screen between them.

To choose this for a display, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open **Settings**, choose **Workspace**, then **Spread**, and set what that display shows: **Automatically**, **One card at a time** or **Side by side**.

## How cards fill the screen

- The first card fills the display.
- A second card stands beside it, and the two share the screen as halves.
- A third goes under whichever half holds fewest, so the screen never splits into thin strips.
- When there is no more room, the pane you used longest ago gives up its place and waits in Spread.

Tapping a card in [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/) adds it beside the card you are using. <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> puts every card into a pane at once.

## Put a window where you want it

Drag a window by its title bar to an edge of the display.

- **The middle of a side edge:** it takes a column on that side, full height.
- **The lower part of a side edge:** it goes under the pane on that side.
- **The upper part of a side edge:** it goes over the pane on that side.

Hold the window still over a place and the panes move aside to show the room it will take, before you let go. While you drag on a monitor, turn the mouse wheel to change its width. A slow turn pauses at common widths.

When a window you dragged leaves room beside it, Shuffle shows pictures of your other cards in that room. Click one to fill the room, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave it empty.

## Drag from another display

Drag a window from another display onto a pane, and rest it where you want it:

- **On the pane's middle:** it joins that pane's [Stack](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/stacks/).
- **On its top or bottom:** it goes above or below it, in that column.
- **On its side:** it starts a new column there.

## Keys

| Press | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> and an arrow key | Moves to the pane that way, or past the edge to the next display. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd>Right</kbd> | Swaps the pane with the one beside it, takes the empty room there, or goes to the next display. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>[</kbd> or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>]</kbd> | Moves the pane's divider. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Up</kbd> | Makes the pane fill the screen. Its layout waits in Spread. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Down</kbd> | Puts a pane that fills the screen back where it was. On a pane in a Stack, takes it out into a pane of its own. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> | Stacks the pane onto the pane you used before it. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Steps through the pane's Stack, in place. |

## Change the sizes

Drag the line between two panes. Where lines meet, drag the crossing to change both at once.

## Stacks in a pane

A pane can hold a Stack. Hold a pane's title bar and drag up, or turn the mouse wheel, to see the title bars of every card in its Stack. Let go to choose one. On a touchscreen, a swipe along the bottom edge steps through it.
