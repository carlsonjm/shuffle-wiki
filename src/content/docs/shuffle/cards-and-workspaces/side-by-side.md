Shuffle can show two or more apps next to each other on one screen. Shuffle calls this **Bento**, and each app in it is a **pane**.

## Start

- Drag a window by its title bar to the left or right edge of the screen and let go. It pairs with the Active card on that side.
- Or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd>. Press it again to end it.

## Adjust

- **Change the sizes:** drag the line between two panes.
- **Swap two panes:** drag one pane onto the other.
- **Take a pane out:** open [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/) and pull that pane's card down. It becomes a card of its own again. Minimizing a pane does the same.

While you drag a window, <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the drag.
