When more than one display is connected, each one has its own cards, its own [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/), its own [Stacks](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/stacks/) and its own side-by-side layout. A card belongs to the display its window is on.

## Which display a key works on

A key such as <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> works on the display with Spread open. If none has Spread open, it works on the display of the window you are using. Search opens on the display you are using.

## Move a card to another display

- **In Spread:** touch and hold a card, or press and hold it with the mouse, until it rises. Carry it to the other display and let it go. It becomes that display's card, or joins its side-by-side layout.
- **With a window:** drag it by its title bar onto the other display. It becomes a card there.
- **With the keyboard:** on a display set to side by side, <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd>Right</kbd> at the edge sends the card to the display that way. See [Cards on a big display](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/big-displays/).

A card always moves with its Stack. On a display that has only one half of a layout showing, the card you send takes the free half.

## The primary display

The primary display is where cards go when nowhere else fits them. To choose it, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open **Settings**, choose **Workspace**, then **Spread**, then **Primary display**. **Automatically** picks the touchscreen if there is one, otherwise the largest display.

## Unplugging a display

When you unplug a display, nothing closes. Its cards wait in Spread on another display, usually the primary one. Open Spread there and pick the one you want.

## One card at a time, or side by side

Each display can show one card at a time, or several side by side. **Automatically**, a small screen shows one card at a time and a big one shows them side by side. To choose for each display, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd>, choose **Workspace**, then **Spread**, and set what each display shows. See [Cards on a big display](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/big-displays/).
