A **Layout** remembers the apps open on a workspace and where each one was. Opening it later starts what isn't running and moves the rest back into place. It never closes anything.

## Save a Layout

1. Open the apps you want, where you want them.
2. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, type **Layouts**, and tap or click **Layouts** in the results.
3. Tap or click **Save what's open**. The apps on this workspace are listed. Tap **Remove** on any you want to leave out.
4. Type a name.
5. To have it open every time you sign in, tap **Open when I sign in**.
6. Tap **Save**.

## Open a Layout

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type the Layout's name, and tap or click **Open** beside it.
- Or open the Layouts list as above, and tap **Open** beside the Layout.

## Pin a Layout to the Actions Panel

In the Layouts list, tap the Layout's name (not its **Open** pill), then tap **Pin to dock**. A tap on its picture in the Actions Panel opens it. To remove it from the panel, touch and hold it, or right-click it, and choose **Unpin**. The Layout stays saved.

## Remove a Layout

In the Layouts list, tap the Layout's name, then tap **Remove**.
