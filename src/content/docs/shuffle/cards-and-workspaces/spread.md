**Spread** shows all your cards in a row, so you can see what is open and pick one.

## Open and close Spread

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>. Press it again to go back to where you were.
- On a touchscreen, swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen. A quick flick opens it fully.
- Put three fingers down on a touchscreen, or four on a touchpad.
- To leave without choosing, press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or tap or click an empty space.

## Use Spread

- **Move along the row:** drag it sideways, turn the mouse wheel, or press the arrow keys.
- **Open a card:** tap or click it, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the card in the middle.
- **Close an app:** flick its card up. An app that asks before closing comes forward instead, so you can answer it.
- **Search:** start typing. Search opens with what you typed.
- **Move a card:** hold it until it rises, then let it go in a gap between two other cards.
- **Move a card to another screen:** hold it, then let it go on the other screen.
- **Stack two cards:** hold one until it rises, then let it go on another card. See [Stack cards together](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/stacks/).
