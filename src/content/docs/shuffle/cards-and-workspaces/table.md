A **workspace** is a separate set of cards, such as one for work and one for home. **Table** shows your workspaces as tabs along the top, with each one's cards hanging under it.

## Open Table

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd>. Press it again to close it.
- Pull down from the top edge of the screen.
- Tap or click the workspace's name at the top left of the screen.

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes Table and leaves everything as it was.

## Go to another workspace

- In Table, tap or click a workspace's tab.
- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Right</kbd> to go one workspace at a time. Up and Down work the same way.
- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>F1</kbd> to <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>F9</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F1</kbd> to <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F9</kbd>, to go straight to workspace 1 to 9.

## Move a card to another workspace

- In Table, pull down past the tabs until the cards hang, then keep pulling until the card under your finger lifts. Let it go on another workspace's tab. Let it go on **+** to start a new workspace with it.
- Or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Right</kbd>. The Active card, with its Stack, moves to the workspace beside.

## Add, rename or remove a workspace

- **Add:** tap or click **+** in Table, then **Empty workspace**. Your saved [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/) are offered there too.
- **Rename:** choose the tab and press <kbd>F2</kbd>, or touch and hold the tab, or right-click it. Type the name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- **Remove:** rename it and clear the name. Table offers to remove it.
