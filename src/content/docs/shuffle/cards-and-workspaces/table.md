A **workspace** is a separate set of cards, such as one for work and one for home. **Table** shows your workspaces as tabs along the top, with each one's cards hanging under it.

## Open Table

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd>. Press it again to close it.
- Pull down from the top edge of the screen.
- Tap or click the workspace's name at the top left of the screen.
- Point the mouse into the top-left corner of a display.
- On a touchscreen, swipe up with three fingers. On a touchpad, use four.

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes Table and leaves everything as it was. So does pushing it back up to the top edge before you let go.

## Keep Table open as a menu bar

Flick Table down and its tabs stay along the top of the screen as a menu bar, so you can keep working with it open. <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd> and the top-left corner open it this way too.

In the menu bar, tap a tab twice to go to that workspace, or tap a card or the preview once to open it. Hover over a tab or card with the mouse to preview it. Drag a card to carry it, as you would in Table.

## Go to another workspace

- In Table, tap or click a workspace's tab. Sliding your finger along the tabs previews each one.
- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Right</kbd> to go one workspace at a time. Up and Down work the same way.
- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>F1</kbd> to <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>F9</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F1</kbd> to <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F9</kbd>, to go straight to workspace 1 to 9.

## Move a card to another workspace

- In Table, pull down past the tabs until the cards hang, then keep pulling until the card under your finger lifts. Let it go on another workspace's tab. Let it go on **+** to start a new workspace with it.
- Or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Right</kbd>. The Active card, with its Stack, moves to the workspace beside. On a side-by-side pane, only that pane goes.

A Stack always moves whole and arrives as a Stack. A pane you carry away leaves its layout.

## Stack cards in Table

Hold a lifted card over another card until they join. They make a Stack, with the card you carried in front. This works across workspaces too.

Rest on a Stack to see its cards hang, the front one marked with a dot. Lift one to open it, or pull it out.

## Use Table with the keyboard

| Press | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The arrow keys | Choose the tab or card beside. <kbd>Down</kbd> goes into a tab's cards or a Stack's, and <kbd>Up</kbd> comes back. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Opens the chosen tab or card. |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> and the arrow keys on a chosen card | Carries it along the tabs and **+**. Let go of <kbd>Shift</kbd> to drop it there. <kbd>Down</kbd> aims at a card to stack it on. |
| <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> on a card in a Stack | Takes it out of the Stack. |
| <kbd>F2</kbd> | Renames the chosen tab. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Closes Table, and puts back a card you were carrying or a name you were typing. |

The mouse wheel steps across the tabs, or across the chosen tab's cards.

## Add, rename or remove a workspace

- **Add:** tap or click **+** in Table, then **Empty workspace**. Your saved [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/) are offered there too.
- **Rename:** choose the tab and press <kbd>F2</kbd>, or touch and hold the tab, or right-click it. Type the name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- **Remove:** rename it and clear the name. Table offers to remove it. Its windows move to the next workspace, or to the one before if it was the last.

## How workspaces get their names, and when they go away

- A new workspace takes the name of the app that made it. One made empty with **+** is called **New** until its first app names it.
- The first workspace on a new install is called **Home**.
- A name you give a workspace keeps it. A name an app gave it does not.
- A workspace you did not name goes away once it is empty and you leave it. The top of the screen says so while you are in it. Signing out never removes one.
