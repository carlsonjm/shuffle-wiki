Instead of letting Shuffle share a big display between cards, you can draw your own **zones** and have each window go into one.

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>T</kbd> to draw the zones for the display you are using. This is Plasma's own zone editor.
2. Switch the display to your zones. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd>, or tap or click the **Cards** icon in the system tray and choose **Monitors use my zones**. To make it last, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open **Settings**, choose **Workspace**, then **Cards**, and set **Arrange windows** to **In my zones**.

Each window then goes into a zone it fits. A window you drag to an edge stays at that edge. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> again to go back to Shuffle arranging the display for you.
