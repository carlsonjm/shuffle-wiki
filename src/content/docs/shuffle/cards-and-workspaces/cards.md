Every app you open becomes a **card**. The card you are using fills the middle of the screen. Shuffle calls it the **Active card**. Your other cards wait on either side of it, in the order you opened them.

## Move to another card

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> for the card before, or <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Right</kbd> for the card after.
- Tap or click the gap to the left or right of the Active card. The card on that side slides in.
- Turn the mouse wheel in the gap beside the Active card. Up brings the card before, and down the card after.
- To see every card at once and pick one, open [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/).

## Make a window a card

Drag the window by its title bar to the top edge of the screen and let go. It becomes the Active card. You can also drag a window onto a screen that shows cards, and it becomes that screen's card.

:::note
To use ordinary overlapping windows for a while instead, see [Turn Cards off](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/plasma-desktop/).
:::
