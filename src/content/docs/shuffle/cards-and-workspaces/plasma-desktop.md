Cards are optional. You can go back to ordinary overlapping windows at any time.

- **For a moment:** press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd>. Every window returns to the ordinary desktop. The next new window you open starts cards again.
- **For one window:** drag the Active card down to the bottom edge of the screen. It becomes an ordinary window where you let go.
- **Until you turn them back on:** tap or click the system tray icon at the top right of the screen, then switch **Cards** off. To bring cards back, switch **Cards** on in the same place.
