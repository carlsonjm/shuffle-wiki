Cards are optional. You can go back to ordinary overlapping windows at any time.

- **For every window:** press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or tap or click the system tray icon at the top right of the screen and switch **Cards** off. Every window returns to the ordinary desktop, and stays that way until you turn Cards back on.
- **To bring cards back:** press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd> again, or switch **Cards** on in the tray. Every window becomes a card again, and the one you were using becomes the Active card.
- **For one window:** drag the Active card down to the bottom edge of the screen, or press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Backspace</kbd>. It becomes an ordinary window, and leaves its Stack if it was in one. Your other cards wait in [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/).
