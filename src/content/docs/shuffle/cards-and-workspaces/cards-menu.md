The **Cards** icon sits in the system tray at the top right of the screen. If you don't see it, open the tray's list of hidden icons. Tap or click it for the Cards menu.

- **Cards on:** turns cards on or off for every display, the same as <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd>. See [Turn Cards off and use ordinary windows](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/plasma-desktop/).
- **Monitors use my zones (Meta+T):** switches a display between Shuffle's own layout and the zones you drew. See [Lay out windows in your own zones](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/zones/).
- **Settings…:** sets the space around the Active card, from none to a wide border. Side-by-side panes stand that far apart too. The same setting is **Space between** in **Settings**, under **Workspace**, then **Cards**.

The menu also says which version of Plasma's window manager Cards was made for. That matters after a Plasma update: see [Cards are off after an update](/shuffle/when-something-goes-wrong/cards-off-after-update/).
