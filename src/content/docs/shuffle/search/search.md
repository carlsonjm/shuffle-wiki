Search is how you open apps, files and settings in Shuffle. What you type stays on this computer.

## Open Search

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> on its own. Press it again to close Search.
- Or tap or click the dot at the left end of the Actions Panel, along the bottom of the screen.
- In [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/), just start typing. Search opens inside Spread with what you typed.

You can add a second key, such as <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Space</kbd>. See [Change how Search looks and opens](/shuffle/search/search-settings/).

## Find something

1. Type. Results appear as you type.
2. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the first result, or use <kbd>Down</kbd> and <kbd>Up</kbd> to pick another. You can also tap or click any result.

Search finds:

- Your apps. An app that is already open is marked **OPEN**, and choosing it brings it forward.
- Settings, such as **Wi-Fi** or **Sound**.
- Your files and folders, and documents that contain the words you typed.
- Your [notes](/shuffle/notes/notes/).
- [Helpers](/shuffle/helpers/helpers/), such as **Check for updates**, and what they know, such as **Storage · 91% full**. Type **install pdf editor**, for example, and choose **Install an app** to open it already searching.
- Your saved [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/), such as **Open your Coding layout**.
- Sums, unit conversions and the time in other places. See [Do sums and conversions](/shuffle/search/sums-and-conversions/).

Some sentences do something instead, such as **remind me to call the bank at 3**. See [quick actions](/shuffle/search/quick-actions/).

Before you type, Search shows the apps and files you opened last. **See all**, beside each row, opens [Apps](/shuffle/search/apps/) or [Files](/shuffle/search/files/).

## Do more with a result

Pick an app or a file with <kbd>Down</kbd> and <kbd>Up</kbd>, and its actions show under it:

- An app's own actions, such as **New Window** or **New Private Window**.
- A file's **Open With…**, **Show in Folder**, **Copy Path** and **Move to Trash**.

Press <kbd>Right</kbd> to move into them, then <kbd>Enter</kbd>. On a touch screen, press and hold a file to pick it without opening it.

A setting can have rows of its own, such as the Bluetooth devices you have connected. Press <kbd>Right</kbd> on the setting to reach them. Choosing one opens its page in Settings. It never connects or changes anything by itself.

Press and hold an app, or right-click it, for its sheet with **Uninstall…**. Nothing is removed until you say yes.

## Go straight to a mode

The row along Search's top holds its **modes**: [Apps](/shuffle/search/apps/), [Files](/shuffle/search/files/), [Notes](/shuffle/notes/notes/), [People](/shuffle/search/people/) and [Ask](/shuffle/search/ask/). Tap one to go straight to it. The dot at the start of the row brings you back to Search's first screen, keeping what you typed.

You can also open a mode from the dot on the Actions Panel. Press the dot and slide toward the islands. The modes line up beside it. Let go on one to open it, filling the screen. Press and hold the dot to see them before you choose.

## Search the web

If nothing on the computer matches, press <kbd>Enter</kbd> and your browser searches the web for what you typed. **Search the web for …**, after the results, does the same at any time. Nothing goes to the web until you choose it.

## Search all files

At first Search looks in your everyday files and leaves out the ones apps keep for themselves. Tap **Search all files**, after the results, to include every file the computer has indexed, and **Search everyday files only** to go back. **Folders to skip…** lists the folders everyday search leaves out. Add a folder on its own line and tap **Save**.

## Close Search

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes an open menu, then a mode, then Search. A tap outside Search, or **Close** at the right end of the modes, closes it too.
