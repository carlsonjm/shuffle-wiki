Search is how you open apps, files and settings in Shuffle. Nothing you type is sent online.

## Open Search

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> on its own. Press it again to close Search.
- Or tap or click the dot at the left end of the Actions Panel, along the bottom of the screen.

## Find something

1. Type. Results appear as you type.
2. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the first result, or use <kbd>Down</kbd> and <kbd>Up</kbd> to pick another. You can also tap or click any result.

If nothing on the computer matches, <kbd>Enter</kbd> searches the web in your browser.

## More you can do

- **Actions on a result:** highlight an app or a file, and its actions show under it, such as **New Window** for an app or **Show in Folder** for a file. Press <kbd>Right</kbd> to move among them.
- **What you used lately:** before you type, Search shows the apps and files you opened last. Tap one to open it.
- **Modes:** the row along Search's top holds [Apps](/shuffle/search/apps/), [Files](/shuffle/search/files/), **Notes**, [People](/shuffle/search/people/) and [Ask](/shuffle/search/ask/). Tap one to go straight to it.
- **Search all files:** by default Search looks in your everyday files. Tap **Search all files** to include every indexed file, and **Folders to skip…** to leave folders out.

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes a menu, then a mode, then Search. A tap outside Search closes it too.

## Change what Search shows

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Search and Ask**, then **Search**. There you can turn **Recent apps**, **Recent files** and each mode on or off.
