Search answers small questions as you type, without opening an app. Nothing is sent online.

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Type one of these:
   - A sum, such as **12 * 7** or **250 / 4**.
   - A conversion, such as **10 km in miles** or **350 g in ounces**.
   - The time somewhere else, such as **time in London**.
3. The answer is the first result.
