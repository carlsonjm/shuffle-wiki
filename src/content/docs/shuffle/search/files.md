**Files** shows your folders and files.

## Open Files

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>.
- Or open Search with <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> and tap or click **Files** in the row along its top.

## Get around

- Tap or click a place, such as **Home**, to open it.
- Tap **Recent** to see the 30 files and folders you used last.
- Tap any part of the path at the top to go back up to that folder. <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Up</kbd> goes up one folder.
- <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Right</kbd> go back and forward.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> lets you type a folder's full path.

## Find a file

Type its name. Files narrows the folder you are in. To look in every folder inside it too, tap **Search inside folders**, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
