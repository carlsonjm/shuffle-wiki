Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Search and Ask**, then **Search**. A picture at the top shows Search as it will look.

## Modes

**Modes** are the places along Search's top. Turn **Files**, **Notes**, **People** or **Ask** on or off. **Apps** is always shown. Drag a mode to change its place in the row. The dot always comes first.

## Sections

**Recent apps** and **Recent files** show the eight apps and files you opened last, before you type. Search grows to fit the ones you turn on. With both off, Search is just the field. Drag them to change which comes first.

**Compact** keeps Search shorter, and what does not fit scrolls inside it.

## Results

**Notes in search results** lists notes with the words you type among the other results. With it off, notes still open from the **Notes** mode.

## The Search pearl and its key

Under **Search pearl**:

- **Looks like** chooses the mark at the start of the Actions Panel that opens Search, a **Pearl** or a **Magnifier**.
- **Opens with** adds a second key beside <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>: **Meta+Space**, **Meta+F**, or **Another key**. For another key, press the keys you want, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop.
- **Keep the indicator still** stops the dot from moving.
