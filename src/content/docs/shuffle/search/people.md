**People** in Search lists the contacts from your phone, each person once. Contacts stay on this computer and are never sent anywhere.

## Turn it on

1. Your phone needs to be linked with this computer through KDE Connect first.
2. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Search and Ask**, then **Search**.
3. Under **People**, turn on your phone's contacts.

## Find someone

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, and tap or click **People** in the row along its top.
2. Type part of a name, a number or an address.
3. Tap or click the person. Their page shows every number and address, with **Text**, **Call**, **Email** and **Copy**.

If two entries look like the same person, their page asks. **Join** makes them one, and **Not the same** keeps them apart.
