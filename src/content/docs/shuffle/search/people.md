**People** in Search lists the contacts from your phone, each person once. Contacts stay on this computer and are never sent anywhere.

## Turn it on

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Search and Ask**, then **Search**.
2. Under **Modes**, turn on **People**.
3. Under **People**, turn on **Contacts from** your phone. If it says **No phone yet**, tap **Link a phone** and link your phone with this computer first. Your phone needs the KDE Connect app.

If the phone is not nearby, the contacts you already have stay, and new ones wait until it is.

## Find someone

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, and tap or click **People** in the row along its top.
2. Type the start of a name, a number or an address.
3. Tap or click the person, or pick them with <kbd>Down</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Their page shows every number and address.

Beside each one:

- **Text** and **Call** go to your phone, which opens Messages or offers the call.
- **Email** opens your email app.
- **Copy** copies the number or address.

**‹ People**, or <kbd>Esc</kbd>, goes back.

## When one person shows twice

If two entries look like the same person, their page asks. **Join** makes them one, and **Not the same** keeps them apart and stops asking. A person you joined has **Separate these cards** on their page to take them apart again.

To undo every join at once, open Shuffle Settings, choose **Search and Ask**, then **Search**, and under **People you joined** tap **Start over**.
