**Ask** answers questions about this computer, such as "why is my disk full?", and points you to the Helper that can fix it. It runs on this computer, and nothing you ask leaves it.

## Ask a question

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Tap or click **Ask**, the last pill in the row along Search's top. The field reads **Ask a question, or find a Helper**.
3. Type your question. When you stop typing, the answer appears above the Helpers it names.

Nothing changes on the computer until you open a Helper or tap **Do it** on the answer.

## The first time

Ask needs a model, a file that does the answering, downloaded once. The first time, the answer's place offers **Download**. If it says it needs llama.cpp first, it offers to install it.

## Turn Ask on or off

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, then choose **Search and Ask**, then **Ask**. With Ask off, the pill in Search reads **Helpers**.
