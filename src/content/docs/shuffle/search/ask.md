**Ask** answers questions about this computer, such as "why is my disk full?", and points you to the [Helper](/shuffle/helpers/helpers/) that can fix it. It runs on this computer, and nothing you ask leaves it.

## Ask a question

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Tap or click **Ask** in the row along Search's top. The field reads **Ask a question, or find a Helper**.
3. Type your question. A question is three words or more, or ends with **?**. When you stop typing, the answer appears above the Helpers it names. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to ask at once.

An answer can take up to a minute on a computer without a fast graphics chip. Until then it says **Looking into it…**.

Nothing changes on the computer until you open a Helper or tap **Do it**.

## What an answer can offer

- **The Helpers it names.** Tap one to open it.
- **Do it.** Some answers offer to do one thing for you, such as keeping the answer as a note. The card shows what it will do and the first words it will write. Only your tap does it, once, and the card then says **Done**, or why it did not work.
- **Open a note.** When the answer comes from something you wrote down, it ends with a pill for that note, such as **Open "Accounts"**. Tap it to open the note.

## The Helpers under Ask

With nothing typed, Ask shows a tile for each Helper, as a reminder that they are there. Tap one to open it. **More** shows the rest, including **Layouts** and **Quick actions**. Every Helper also opens from Search's first screen, by typing its name.

## The first time

Ask needs a model, a file that does the answering, downloaded once. The first time you ask, the answer's place names the model, its size, and offers **Download**. If the computer needs **llama.cpp** first, which runs the model, it offers **Install llama.cpp**. **Not now** waits until you clear the field.

## Codex, for bigger fixes

If Codex is installed on this computer and you have a ChatGPT plan, Codex can take bigger questions. It runs online in its own window, and your plan pays for what it does. Shuffle never sees your password, your account or your bill.

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Search and Ask**, then **Ask**. Under **Codex, for bigger fixes**, tap **Sign in**. An answer then ends with **Ask Codex**. Only your tap sends the question on.

- **Who answers** chooses between the model on this computer **with Codex**, which is the usual choice, and **Codex only**, which needs no download. Warnings about scams and passwords always come from this computer.
- **Hand bigger questions to Codex** lets Ask pass a bigger question on by itself, and the answer says so.

## Change Ask

On the same page:

- **Ask in Search** turns Ask on or off. With Ask off, the mode in Search is **Helpers** alone.
- **The model** shows whether it is downloaded. **Remove** deletes it to free the space. The Helpers keep working.
- **Notes kept from Ask** lists the notes Ask wrote for you. Each one is yours to read or delete.
