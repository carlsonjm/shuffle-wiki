**Apps** lists every app on the computer.

## Open Apps

- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>.
- Or open Search with <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> and tap or click **Apps** in the row along its top.

Type to narrow the list by name. Tap or click an app to open it, or bring it forward if it is already open. The arrow keys move between apps, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the one chosen.

**Expand**, at the top right, makes Apps fill the screen, and tapping it again returns it to Search's size.

## Arrange Apps

- **Order:** tap the pill at the right of the row above the apps, then choose **A to Z**, **Most used** or **Newest installed**.
- **Size:** tap **−** or **+**, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>+</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>−</kbd>, or pinch. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>0</kbd> returns to the usual size.
- **Make a folder:** drag one app onto another. Or tap **New folder**, choose two or more apps, and tap **Make folder**.
- **Open or close a folder:** tap or click it. Its apps open in place, under its row.

## Folders

A folder looks like a paper folder of the kind its apps share, such as a green one for games or a gold one with a briefcase for office apps. When the apps share no kind, it is plain.

Touch and hold a folder, or right-click it, for its sheet:

- **Its nine looks:** tap one to draw the folder that way from now on.
- **Rename:** type the new name, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or tap elsewhere. <kbd>Esc</kbd> keeps the old one.
- **Remove folder:** puts its apps back in Apps.

To put an app in a folder, drag it onto the folder, or choose the folder in the app's sheet. **Take out of folder** in the sheet takes it out again. A folder left with one app goes away.

Drag a folder onto the Actions Panel to pin all its apps there as one folder of the same name.

## Pin, hide or remove an app

Touch and hold an app, or right-click it. Its sheet offers:

- **Pin to dock**, to keep it on the Actions Panel. **Unpin from dock** removes it.
- **Hide**, to take it out of Apps and what you used lately. Search still finds it. To see hidden apps, tap the eye inside the search field. They show dimmed, and you can unhide one from its sheet.
- **Its own actions**, such as a new window.
- **Edit Application…**, to change the name, icon or command that app uses for you. This opens KDE's menu editor.

To uninstall an app, open Search with <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type its name, then touch and hold it in the results, or right-click it, and choose **Uninstall…**. Nothing is removed until you confirm.
