Some things can be done straight from Search by typing what you want.

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Type a sentence, such as one of these:
   - **remind me to check the oven at 6**
   - **note: milk**
   - **do not disturb until 3**
   - **bluetooth off**
   - **keep awake 1h**
3. The first result says what will happen. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or tap it.

A reminder or a note opens in Notes, ready to keep. A switch, such as Bluetooth, changes right there, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> puts it back.

## See every quick action

Open Search, type **Quick actions**, and tap or click **Quick actions** in the results. A list shows what to type for each one.
