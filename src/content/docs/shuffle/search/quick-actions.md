Some things can be done straight from Search by typing what you want. The first result says what will happen, and nothing happens until you press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or tap it.

## What you can type

| To | Type, for example |
| --- | --- |
| Set a [reminder](/shuffle/notes/reminders/) | **remind me to call the bank at 3** |
| Write a [note](/shuffle/notes/notes/) | **note: router password is on the box** |
| Turn on [Do not disturb](/shuffle/around-the-screen/do-not-disturb/) | **do not disturb until 3** |
| [Keep the screen awake](/shuffle/around-the-screen/keep-awake/) | **keep awake 1h** |
| Turn Bluetooth off or on | **bluetooth off** |

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Type the sentence.
3. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or tap the first result.

## Reminders and notes

A reminder or a note opens on its note card, with the words in it and the time already set, so you can look it over. A reminder understands days such as **tomorrow** or **on Friday**, times such as **at 4:30pm** or **at noon**, and spans such as **in 20 minutes**. A day with no time is set for 9 AM, or for the **Default reminder time** you choose below.

To choose another time before you press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, press <kbd>Tab</kbd>, or tap **change**. It offers the time you typed, this evening and tomorrow morning.

## Switches

**Do not disturb**, **Keep awake** and **Bluetooth** change right there in Search, which stays open and says what it did. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>, or tap **Undo**, to put it back. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> again to close Search.

Typing **dnd** or **keep awake** alone turns it the other way from where it is now. If it is already as you asked, Search says so and changes nothing.

## See every quick action

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, type **Quick actions**, and tap or click **Quick actions** in the results. Its island lists what to type for each one. Tap **Try** beside one to start it in Search.

## Change quick actions

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Search and Ask**, then **Quick actions**. You can turn quick actions off, choose **Where the action shows**, let switches open their settings page instead with **Open its page instead**, and set the **Default reminder time** for a day with no time.
