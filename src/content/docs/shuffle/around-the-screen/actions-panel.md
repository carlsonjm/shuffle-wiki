The bar along the bottom of the screen is the **Actions Panel**, also called the dock. From left to right it holds the dot that opens Search, the pearls for Spread, Table and the on-screen keys, your apps and folders, and the [islands](/shuffle/around-the-screen/islands/).

## Use an app's icon

- **Tap or click** an app to bring it forward, or start it. Tap the app in front to minimize it.
- **Touch and hold, or right-click,** an app to open its sheet: its windows, **New window**, the workspaces, and **Close all**.
- **Middle-click**, or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + click, opens a new window.
- <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>9</kbd> act on the first nine places, counted from the left.
- Drop files on an icon to open them in that app.

## Arrange it

- **Reorder:** drag an icon sideways.
- **Pin an app:** press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd> to open Apps, touch and hold the app, or right-click it, and choose **Pin to dock**. Or drag it from Apps onto the panel.
- **Make a folder:** drag one icon onto another and rest a moment on its middle before letting go. Touch and hold a folder, or right-click it, to **Rename** or **Take apart**.
- **Unpin an app:** press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd> to open Apps, touch and hold the app, or right-click it, and choose **Unpin from dock**.

## Change it

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Actions**: **Pearls**, **Size and icons**, **Apps and folders** and **Islands**.
