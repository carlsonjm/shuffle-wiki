**Keep awake** stops the screen from dimming or locking, and stops the computer from going to sleep on its own. Closing the lid, the power button and **Sleep** still work as usual.

## Turn it on or off

Tap or click the network icon near the top right of the screen to open [Control Center](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/), then tap **Keep awake**. Tap it again to turn it off.

Turned on this way, it lasts until you turn it off or sign out.

## Keep awake for a while

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, then type one of these:

- **keep awake 1h**
- **keep awake until 5**
- **keep awake for 20 minutes**

Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or tap **Turn on**. In Control Center, the time it ends shows under **Keep awake**, such as **Until 5:00 PM**, and it turns itself off then. To undo it at once, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> or tap **Undo**. See [quick actions](/shuffle/search/quick-actions/).

## If Keep awake is not in Control Center

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Control Center**. Under **Where each icon shows**, set **Keep awake** to **Control Center**.
