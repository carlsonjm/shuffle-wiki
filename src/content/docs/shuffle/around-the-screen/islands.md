**Islands** are the small pills beside Search's dot on the Actions Panel. Each shows one thing that is going on: music playing, a download, a drive you plugged in, an app waiting for you, a quick note, or something a Helper wants you to know.

- **Tap or click** an island to open it larger, with its controls.
- **Play, pause and skip** music right on its island.
- **Flick an island sideways** to set it aside. Music comes back when it plays again.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or a tap outside, closes an open island.

## The quick note

The island with a **+** starts a quick note. Type, then tap **Done**. **Open in Notes** moves it to Notes.

## Change which islands show

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Actions**, then **Islands**.
