**Islands** are the small pills beside Search's dot on the [Actions Panel](/shuffle/around-the-screen/actions-panel/). Each one shows one thing that is going on, and leaves when it is over.

## Use an island

- **Tap or click** an island to open it larger. Every open island has its title, what it holds, and its buttons, ending in **Close**.
- **Flick an island sideways** to set it aside. Drag it slowly and it sheds its details, and it springs back if you let go early.
- With an island chosen with the keyboard, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens it and <kbd>Delete</kbd> sets it aside.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or a tap outside, closes an open island.

When there is not room for every island, the ones that cannot fit fold into a round **bubble** after the others, which shows how many it holds. Tap the bubble to open what it holds.

## What each island shows

- **Music and video.** Play, pause and skip right on the island. Open it to move along the track, to switch between players, or to tap **Show** and bring the player forward. Set aside, it comes back when something plays again.
- **Copies and downloads.** A ring shows how far along it is. Tap **×** to cancel. Open it to **Pause transfer** or **Resume**. When a download finishes, the island shows **Show in Files**, which opens [Files](/shuffle/search/files/) with the new file chosen.
- **A drive you plug in.** Tap it, or its **Open**, and Files opens the drive. See [drives](/shuffle/files/drives/).
- **An app waiting for you.** When an app needs an answer while it is not in front, its island shows its name and question. Tap it to bring the app and its question forward.
- **A shared screen.** While an app shares or records your screen, a red dot shows, with who receives it. **Stop** ends the share.
- **Something a Helper wants you to know,** such as the disk filling up. Tap it to open the [Helper](/shuffle/helpers/helpers/) that deals with it. Some ask a question, such as whether to bring back your apps from last time, and open with the answers. Set aside, a Helper stays quiet about it until it gets worse.
- **What came in during [Do not disturb](/shuffle/around-the-screen/do-not-disturb/).** Tap it to open your notifications.

## The quick note and Layouts

Two islands stay even when nothing is going on, because they are doors rather than news.

- **The quick note.** Tap the **+** to write a quick note, then tap **Done**. **Open in Notes** moves it to Notes. A note you pin in Notes shows here too, and tapping it opens it. When the window in front has [notes stuck to it](/shuffle/notes/window-notes/), a dot shows on the island; tap it to bring those notes up.
- **Layouts.** The disc opens your saved [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/). Tap **Open** beside one to open it, or **Save what's open** to keep what is on screen now as a new Layout. Tap a saved Layout off its **Open** pill for **Pin to dock** and **Remove**. It shows only while you have a Layout that fits the displays you have connected.

## Change which islands show

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Actions**, then **Islands**.

- **Islands**, **Show** or **Hide**, turns them all on or off. Hidden, Search's dot stands alone.
- **Notes island** and **Layouts island** show or hide those two.
- **Each island sits** chooses **In a pill** or **On the panel**, and **Spacing** how far apart they are.
- **Islands show** chooses **Everything**, **Music only** or **All but music**, and **Islands gather** where they sit. When your panels are set up with Search's dot in two places, **Music sits before the dock, the rest after it** shows in their place instead.
