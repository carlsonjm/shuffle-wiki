Apps that keep running in the background, such as a chat app or a cloud drive, put a small icon in the **system tray**. The tray sits near the top right of the screen, in the [Context Panel](/shuffle/around-the-screen/context-panel/).

## Use a tray icon

Tap or click the tray icon to open the system tray. Then:

- Tap an entry to open it, or to do what its app says.
- Right-click, or press and hold, an entry for its own menu.
- Middle-click an entry for its second action, where the app has one.
- Scroll over an entry to pass the scroll to it, such as to change the volume.

## Choose where each icon shows

Each icon can sit in the tray, show as a button in [Control Center](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/), or, for some, not show at all.

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Control Center**. Under **Where each icon shows**, choose **Tray**, **Control Center** or **Off** for each one. Apps show in this list only while they are running.

## The battery

On a laptop or tablet, the battery and how much charge is left sit at the right end of the panel. Tap or click the battery to open the **Power and Battery** page.

To change when the screen dims and the computer sleeps, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Power and battery**.
