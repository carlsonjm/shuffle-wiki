## The calendar

Tap or click the clock at the top of the screen. The calendar opens on this month.

- Tap a day to see its holidays and events.
- Swipe sideways, or use the arrows, to change the month. **Today** comes back to today.

To show your own calendar's events, copy its private address from your calendar service, then press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Clock**. Under **Calendars**, paste the address and tap **Add**.

## The weather

Tap or click the weather icon near the top right of the screen. To change the units or turn the online weather off, open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Weather**.
