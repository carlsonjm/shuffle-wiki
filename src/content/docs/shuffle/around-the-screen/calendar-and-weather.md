## Open the calendar

Tap or click the clock at the top of the screen. The calendar opens on this month. Tap the clock again, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to close it.

- Tap a day to see its holidays and events.
- Swipe sideways across the days, or use the arrows, to change the month. A mouse wheel or a touchpad scroll changes it too.
- **Today** comes back to today. It shows only when you are looking at another month.
- **Smaller calendar** shows the month alone. **Larger calendar** shows the month with the next few days.

## Show your own calendar's events

Shuffle reads a calendar through its private address. It does not change the calendar, and it checks for new events every ten minutes. Anyone who has the address can read the calendar, so keep it to yourself.

1. Copy the calendar's private address from your calendar service. Google Calendar calls it the **secret address in iCal format**. iCloud and Outlook give you one when you share or publish a calendar.
2. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Clock**.
3. Under **Calendars**, paste the address into **Private calendar address** and tap **Add**.

The calendar's events show in the calendar, and today's events scroll past beside the bell along the top. If Shuffle cannot read the address, it says **Couldn't read this calendar. Check the address.**

Each linked calendar has a colour, which its events' dots take. To change it, choose a new colour beside the calendar's name. **Remove** unlinks the calendar.

## Change the clock

In Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Clock**. You can choose:

- **Date**, the way the date shows beside the time, or **None**.
- **Hours**, **12-hour** or **24-hour**, or **Region** to follow your region's way, and how **AM and PM** show.
- **Seconds**, to show them.
- **Clock sits**, at the **Left**, **Center** or **Right** of the panel.
- **Size**, and under **Font and size**, the clock's **Font**.

## The weather

Tap or click the weather icon near the top right of the screen. The weather page opens with the forecast. **Open weather** opens your weather app.

To change it, open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Weather**:

- **Show the weather** puts the icon in the panel, or takes it away.
- **Temperature** chooses **Fahrenheit** or **Celsius**.
- **Use online weather** fetches the forecast when the computer has no weather of its own. It sends your rough location to the weather service.
