## Lock

Tap the network icon at the top right of the screen to open [Control Center](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/). Tap **More** at its top, then tap **Lock**. The screen locks at once.

## Unlock or sign in

Type your password and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. On a touchscreen, tap the password box, or the keyboard button at the bottom right, and the on-screen keys come up.

When the computer has more than one account, tap yours first. **Other accounts** goes back to the list.

## Sleep, restart or shut down from here

Tap **Sleep**, **Restart** or **Shut down**. Restart and Shut down ask you to tap a second time within three seconds, so a stray tap does nothing.
