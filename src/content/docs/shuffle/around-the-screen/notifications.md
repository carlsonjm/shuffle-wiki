Notifications collect under the **bell** near the top right of the screen. The newest one scrolls past beside it.

## See and clear notifications

- Tap or click the bell. The list opens.
- Tap a notification to open what it is about.
- Tap **Dismiss** to remove one, or **Clear** beside an app to remove all of that app's.
- Flick the scrolling line beside the bell sideways to check it off without opening the list.

## Do not disturb

Tap the bell, then **Do not disturb**. Tap it again to turn it off. You can also type **do not disturb until 3** in Search; see [quick actions](/shuffle/search/quick-actions/).

## Change notifications

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Notifications**. **Each app** lets you quiet one app at a time.
