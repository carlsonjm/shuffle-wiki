Notifications collect under the **bell** near the top right of the screen, in the [Context Panel](/shuffle/around-the-screen/context-panel/). The newest one, and today's events from your [calendar](/shuffle/around-the-screen/calendar-and-weather/), scroll past on one line beside the bell. This line is the **ticker**.

## Open the list

Tap or click the bell, or the ticker's line. **Notifications & Events** opens with everything that came in. Opening it counts as reading them, so the ticker clears.

- Tap a notification to do what it offers, or to open the app that sent it. A pill on a notification, such as **Reply**, does only that.
- Tap **Dismiss** on a notification to remove it.
- Tap **Dismiss** on one of today's events to hide it until tomorrow.
- Tap **Clear** beside an app to remove that app's notifications. **Clear** beside **Recent** removes every notification, and today's events stay.
- Tap **Expand** or **Collapse** on an app to open or fold its group. **Show more** opens a long notification in place.
- Tap the bell again, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to close the list.

## Check one off without opening it

Flick the ticker's line sideways. The notification waits unread in the list, and the next line comes up. The bell counts what is still waiting, then shows a tick until you open the list. Let go early and the line springs back.

To see the latest line again, rest the pointer on the bell, or flick sideways over the empty ticker.

## Urgent banners

Calls, alarms and warnings that cannot wait come down from the top as a **banner**.

- Tap the banner to do what it offers.
- **Dismiss** removes it.
- **Keep for review** moves it to the list under the bell.
- **Show more** moves it to the list and opens it there.

## Change what each app may send

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Notifications**. Under **Each app**, tap an app to see its switches:

- **Show its notifications.** Turned off, the app's notifications go straight to the list without appearing along the top.
- **Play a sound.** Plays the sound the app sends with a notification, if it sends one.
- **Keep it in the list afterwards.**
- **Show while Do not disturb is on.** Lets this app through [Do not disturb](/shuffle/around-the-screen/do-not-disturb/).

An app appears under **Each app** after it sends its first notification. **KDE's own parts** holds the notifications from power, networks, updates and the rest of the system.

## Change how notifications show

On the same page:

- **Waiting shows as** chooses whether the bell shows a **Count** of what is waiting, or just the **Bell**.
- **Ticker** chooses whether the line beside the bell **Scrolls** or stays **Still**.
- **Urgent banner** turns the banners on or off, and **Banner stays** sets how long one stays, from one second to two minutes, or **Until dismissed**.
- **Quiet while sharing** keeps notifications away while an app shares your screen. See [Do not disturb](/shuffle/around-the-screen/do-not-disturb/).
- **Show volume and brightness changes on screen** turns the small pop-up for those changes on or off.
