While **Do not disturb** is on, notifications do not appear along the top or scroll past. They still collect in the list under the bell, and the bell shows a line through it.

## Turn it on or off

Tap or click the bell near the top right of the screen, then tap **Do not disturb**. Tap it again to turn it off.

Turned on this way, it stays on until you turn it off, even after you sign out and back in.

## Turn it on until a time

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, then type one of these:

- **do not disturb until 3**
- **dnd for 20 minutes**
- **dnd 1h**

The first result says what it will change. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or tap **Turn on**. Do not disturb turns itself off at that time, even with Search closed. To undo it at once, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> or tap **Undo**. Typing **dnd** alone turns it the other way from where it is now. See [quick actions](/shuffle/search/quick-actions/).

## See what came in while it was on

When Do not disturb ends, an [island](/shuffle/around-the-screen/islands/) on the Actions Panel says how many notifications came in. Tap it to open the list.

The list then starts with **While you were focused**, with how many came in and between which times. Those notifications have a coloured edge. Everything else follows under **Earlier**.

If you turn Do not disturb off from the list itself, no island appears, because you are already looking at what came in.

## Let one app through

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Notifications**. Under **Each app**, tap the app and turn on **Show while Do not disturb is on**.

## Quiet while you share your screen

With **Quiet while sharing** turned on in the same place, no notifications appear while an app shares your screen. The bell shows a line through it, and the island for the shared screen says **Notifications quiet**. Afterwards, the island says **While you were sharing** and what came in. Turning Do not disturb off during the share brings notifications back at once.
