**Control Center** holds the everyday switches and sliders.

## Open it

Tap or click the network icon near the top right of the screen.

## What is in it

- **Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the other switches.** Tap one to turn it on or off.
- **Volume and brightness.** Drag the slider. Tap the icon beside it for the full page.
- **Keep awake.** Stops the computer from sleeping while it is on.
- **The performance profile.** Tap it to choose how the computer balances speed against battery, and to set where charging stops.
- **Sleep, Lock, Restart and Shut down** along the bottom. **More** holds **Log out** and **Switch user**.

**Back** returns to the previous page. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

## Change it

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Control Center**. You can change the order of the switches and the power row.
