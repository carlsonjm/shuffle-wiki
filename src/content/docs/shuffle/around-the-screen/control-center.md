**Control Center** holds the everyday switches and sliders.

## Open it

Tap or click the network icon near the top right of the screen.

## What is in it

- **Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the other switches.** Tap one to turn it on or off.
- **Volume and brightness.** Drag the slider. Tap the icon beside it for the full page.
- **[Keep awake](/shuffle/around-the-screen/keep-awake/).** Stops the screen from dimming and the computer from sleeping while it is on.
- **The performance profile,** on computers that support it. Tap it to choose how the computer balances speed against battery, and to set where charging stops. If your computer does not support it, the entry does not appear.

## Sleep, restart and shut down

The buttons sit along Control Center's top edge. **Shut down** is always there. **Sleep** and **Restart** sit beside it when there is room. **More** holds the rest: **Lock** and **Log out**, and **Switch user** if you have turned it on.

Lock and Sleep happen at once. Restart, Shut down and Log out ask first.

**Back** returns to the previous page. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

## Change it

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Context**, then **Control Center**. You can change the order of the switches, and in **Power row** the order of the buttons along the top and whether **Switch user** appears. **Where each icon shows** decides which app icons sit in the [system tray](/shuffle/around-the-screen/system-tray/) and which show as buttons here.
