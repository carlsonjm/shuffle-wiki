**Health check** looks the computer over and says, row by row, what is fine and what needs a look. It only reads. It changes nothing.

## Run it

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, type **Health check**, and tap it. **Check again** looks once more. **Close** closes it.

## What it looks at

- **Space:** how much room is left on the disk. When it is low, it offers [Free up space](/apps/devices-and-files/free-up-space/).
- **Updates:** whether updates are waiting. It offers [Check for updates](/shuffle/helpers/check-for-updates/).
- **Services:** whether everything that runs in the background started.
- **Restore points:** whether [restore points](/shuffle/helpers/restore-points/) are being taken, and how old the newest one is.
- **Drive:** whether the drive reports a problem, or that it is wearing out.
- **Battery,** on a laptop or tablet: how much of its original charge it still holds, and any fault it reports.
- **Crashes:** apps that crashed this week.
- **Installing:** whether an update was cut off part way.
- **Installed apps:** whether the list of what is installed has a problem.
- **Restart:** whether the computer needs a restart to finish an update.
- **Settings files:** new settings files that an update left beside yours.
- **Old downloads** and **Old copies:** how much room old downloads and old copies of updates take.

## When a row needs a look

A row that a Helper can fix offers that Helper. Tap it.

A row that no Helper fixes offers **Copy for help**. It copies the problem, written as a question, with the computer's make and model and its versions, to paste into a forum or a message. Nothing is sent anywhere. Some rows offer **Ask about it**, which asks [Ask](/shuffle/search/ask/) instead.

If the drive reports a problem, copy your files somewhere safe soon. See [Back up your photos](/apps/devices-and-files/back-up-photos/).

## It also looks by itself

Once a week, the Helpers look the computer over in the background. A new problem shows along the top of the screen, and a failing drive on an island. See [When Helpers speak up](/shuffle/helpers/when-helpers-speak-up/).
