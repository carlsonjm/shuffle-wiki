1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, type **What changed**, and tap it.
2. Find the change in the list.
3. Tap **Undo** beside it.

The change is put back as it was. If a file has changed since, it is left as it is and nothing is put back.

Some rows offer more:

- **Put back**, on a setting that changed. It asks first, with **Show details** for the exact values. **Do it** puts the setting back, and **Show me how instead** opens its page in Settings.
- **Start it again**, on a background service that stopped. It shows what the service said last.
- **Go back**, on an app from Flathub that was updated. It takes the app back to the version before.
- **Open in Settings** opens the page for that setting.
- **Copy for help** copies exactly what changed, with what the computer is, to paste wherever you ask for help.

**Look again** reads the list once more.

To go back further, open **Restore points** from Search the same way.
