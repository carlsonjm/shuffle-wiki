The Helpers watch the computer quietly in the background. When something needs a look, they say so once, along the top of the screen in the [ticker](/shuffle/around-the-screen/notifications/) or on an [island](/shuffle/around-the-screen/islands/) when it matters more. Tap the message to open the Helper that fits.

## What they tell you about

| When | What you see | Tap it to open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The disk is 75% full | **Storage** along the top, such as **Storage · 76% full** | Free up space |
| The disk is 90% full, or filling fast | An island | Free up space |
| Updates have waited 7 days | **Updates** along the top, such as **Updates · 12 waiting** | Check for updates |
| Updates have waited 21 days | An island | Check for updates |
| The weekly look finds a problem | **Health** along the top | Health check |
| The drive reports it is failing | An island | Health check |
| A Windows program (.exe or .msi) lands in Downloads | An island, such as **ZoomInstaller.exe is for Windows** | Install an app, searching for the Linux version |
| An update needs a restart | **Restart to finish an update** along the top | The restart question |
| A Shuffle update needs you to sign out | **Sign out to finish the Shuffle update** along the top | The sign-out question |
| Downloads and the Desktop pass 5 GB | **Downloads** along the top | Tidy up Downloads |
| A new printer, drive, Bluetooth device, game controller or screen arrives | **New printer · Set it up**, or the like, along the top | Its setup page |
| Do not disturb ends | An island: **While you were focused** | Your notifications |

Nothing restarts or signs out without you: tapping the message only asks.

## How often

- Each thing is said once, then again only when it gets worse, such as the disk getting fuller or updates waiting another week.
- **Not now**, or setting the island aside, quiets it until it gets worse.
- Each topic takes the island at most once a day. While [Do not disturb](/shuffle/around-the-screen/do-not-disturb/) is on, messages wait or go along the top only.
- An island nobody touches goes away after a day.

## Change what they tell you about

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Helpers**.

- **Speak up** turns the messages about a filling drive, waiting updates and problems on or off. Off, every Helper still opens from Search.
- **Pings for** moves the marks together: **Less** waits until the disk is fuller and updates are further behind, **More** speaks up sooner. The line beneath says the marks it uses now.
- **Health check**, **Windows programs**, **Tidy up Downloads**, **New devices** and **After Do Not Disturb** each turn one kind of message on or off.
