**Helpers** look after the computer for you. Each does one job, in plain words, in a window of its own. It looks first, and changes nothing until you tap its button.

## Open a Helper

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, type the Helper's name, such as **Free up space**, and tap it. Every Helper opens this way.

To be reminded which Helpers there are, tap **Ask** in the row along Search's top. The Helpers sit below the place to ask, one tile each. **More** shows the rest.

**Close**, **Not now**, or closing the window closes a Helper. A Helper that is changing something stays open until it is done.

## The Helpers

| Helper | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Check for updates](/shuffle/helpers/check-for-updates/) | Lists every update before it starts, then installs them, with a restore point first. |
| [Health check](/shuffle/helpers/health-check/) | Looks the computer over and says what is fine and what needs a look. |
| [What changed](/shuffle/helpers/undo-a-change/) | Shows the last two weeks of changes, with a way to undo each one. |
| [Restore points](/shuffle/helpers/restore-points/) | Shows the saved points the computer can go back to. |
| **Your apps** | Lists the apps you added, biggest first, with a way to remove each one. |
| **Install an app** | Finds the app for what you need, such as a PDF editor or a photo editor, or the one you name. |
| [Free up space](/apps/devices-and-files/free-up-space/) | Names everything it would remove before it removes any of it. |
| **Tidy up Downloads** | Gathers old downloads, so you can move them to the Trash. See [Free up space](/apps/devices-and-files/free-up-space/). |
| [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/) | Saves and reopens a set of apps. |

## Remove an app

Touch and hold an app in Search or in [Apps](/shuffle/search/apps/), or right-click it, then choose **Uninstall…**. **Remove an app** opens on that app, shows what will be removed, and removes nothing until you say yes. The parts the computer itself runs on are never offered. See also [Remove an app](/apps/getting-apps/remove-app/).

## What a Helper writes down

Before a Helper changes anything, it writes what it is about to change, and how to undo it, in your **notebook**. The notebook is an ordinary folder, **Notebook**, in your **Documents** folder. The file **This computer** in it is the full record, in plain words. [What changed](/shuffle/helpers/undo-a-change/) reads it, so you can undo a change with one tap.

## When the computer needs a look

When space runs low, updates have waited too long, or something new turns up, a Helper says so along the top or on an island. Tap it to open the Helper that fits. See [When Helpers speak up](/shuffle/helpers/when-helpers-speak-up/).

## What a Helper never does

- Show you a terminal.
- Change anything without asking first.
- Hold your password. Anything that needs one uses the system's own password prompt.
- Ask for money or card details.
