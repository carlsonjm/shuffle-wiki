**Helpers** look after the computer for you. Each does one job, in plain words, and changes nothing until you tap its button. Before it changes anything, it writes down what it will change and how to undo it.

## Open a Helper

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search and type the Helper's name, such as **Free up space**, then tap it. To see them all, tap **Ask** (or **Helpers**) in the row along Search's top.

## The Helpers

| Helper | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Check for updates** | Lists every update before it starts, then installs them, with a restore point first. |
| **Health check** | Looks the computer over and says what is fine and what needs a look. |
| **What changed** | Shows the last two weeks of changes, with a way to undo each one. See [Undo a change](/shuffle/helpers/undo-a-change/). |
| **Restore points** | Shows the saved points the computer can go back to. |
| **Your apps** | Lists the apps you added, biggest first. |
| **Install an app** | Finds the app for what you need. |
| **Remove an app** | Removes the app you name. |
| **Free up space** | Names everything it would remove before it removes any of it. |
| **Tidy up Downloads** | Moves old downloads to the Trash. |
| **Layouts** | Saves and reopens a set of apps. See [Layouts](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/layouts/). |

## When the computer needs a look

When space runs low, updates have waited too long, or a new device is plugged in, an island on the Actions Panel says so. Tap it to open the Helper that fits. Flick it sideways to set it aside.

To change what the Helpers tell you about, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Helpers**.

A Helper never asks for money or card details, and never holds your password. Anything that needs a password uses the system's own prompt.
