---
title: "Shuffle won't start after sign-in"
description: "Choose “KDE Plasma (turns Shuffle off)” at the sign-in screen."
keywords: "broken black screen wont start crash sign in login plain plasma"
tableOfContents: false
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. Restart the computer and wait for the sign-in screen.
2. Before typing your password, open the list of sessions on the sign-in screen and choose **KDE Plasma (turns Shuffle off)**.
3. Sign in. You have plain Plasma, and your apps and files are as they were.
