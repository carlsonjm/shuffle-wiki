Try these in order. Each one goes a step further. Anything unsaved in a closed app is lost.

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd>, as on Windows. System Monitor opens. Click **Applications** on the left, click the frozen app, then **Quit Application**, and **Quit Application** again to confirm.
2. Still stuck? Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Esc</kbd>. The pointer changes. Click the frozen window and it closes. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.
3. The whole screen is stuck: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Del</kbd>, then choose **Restart**.
4. Nothing responds at all: hold the power button for about ten seconds until the computer turns off, then turn it on again.
