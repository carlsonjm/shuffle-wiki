On a touchscreen, the **Shuffle Keyboard** comes up when you tap a text field, and goes away when you leave it.

## Bring it up or put it away

- Tap a text field. The keys rise from the bottom of the screen.
- Or tap **Keyboard** in [Control Center](/shuffle/around-the-screen/control-center/) or the [system tray](/shuffle/around-the-screen/system-tray/), or the keyboard pearl on the Actions Panel. Tap it again to put the keys away.
- Tap **Hide** on the keys to put them away.

The keys also come up on the lock screen and the sign-in screen, where they are always dark. In a terminal, tap the window to bring them up.

## Type

- **Letters:** a key types as your finger lifts. Two thumbs can overlap, and the letters stay in order.
- **Capitals:** tap **Shift** for one capital. Double-tap it for Caps Lock, and tap it again to turn Caps Lock off.
- **Accents:** hold a, e, i, o, u, n, c, s or y. Its accents appear above it. Slide to one and lift.
- **Numbers and symbols:** flick a key down to type the small grey character above it. Or tap **123** for the symbols, and **ABC** to come back.
- **Emoji:** tap **123**, then **Emoji**. Swipe across the panel, or tap a category. **ABC** brings the letters back.
- **Enter:** tap **Go**. Tap **Shift**, then **Go**, for a new line without sending, in most chat boxes.
- **Tab** moves to the next field. **Esc** is on the symbols layer, or flick **123** down.
- **Search:** the dot key opens what <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> opens, which is Search.

## Flick down for numbers and symbols

| Flick down on | To type |
| --- | --- |
| The top row, Q to P | 1 to 0 |
| The middle row, A to L | `! @ # $ % ^ & * (` |
| The bottom row, Z to M | `` ) ~ ` € £ § … `` |
| Comma, period and slash | `< > ?` |

As your finger moves down, the grey character grows into the key. A short move, or a move mostly sideways, types the key's own letter instead.

## Ctrl and Alt

Tap **Ctrl** or **Alt**, then a key. It lets go after that key.

- **Ctrl**, then **C**, **X**, **V** or **Z**: copy, cut, paste or undo.
- **Ctrl**, **Shift**, then **C**: copy in a terminal.
- Double-tap **Ctrl** or **Alt** to keep it on for every key, until you tap it again. For example, double-tap **Ctrl** and tap **Z** three times to undo three steps.

## Delete

- Tap **Delete** to delete one character.
- Hold **Delete** to keep deleting, first by characters, then by words.
- Flick left from **Delete** to delete the word before the cursor.
- Drag left from **Delete** to mark characters, then lift to delete them. Slide back to take marks off.

## Move the cursor, or use the keys as a touchpad

See [Move the cursor with the keys](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/keyboard-trackpad/).

## With a keyboard plugged in

Holding a key on a plugged-in keyboard repeats it as usual. The accents are only on the on-screen keys.

## What it does not do

The Shuffle Keyboard has no autocorrect, word suggestions, swipe typing or dictation. The emoji panel has no search.
