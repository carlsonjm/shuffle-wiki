On a touchscreen, the **Shuffle Keyboard** comes up when you tap a text field, and goes away when you leave it.

## Bring it up or put it away

- Tap a text field.
- Or tap **Keyboard** in Control Center or the system tray, or the keyboard pearl on the Actions Panel.
- Tap **Hide** on the keys to put them away.

## Typing

- **Capitals:** tap **Shift** for one capital. Double-tap it for Caps Lock.
- **Accents:** hold a, e, i, o, u, n, c, s or y, slide to the accent, and lift.
- **Numbers and symbols:** flick a key down to type the small grey character above it, such as the numbers along the top row. Or tap **123** for the symbols layer, and **ABC** to come back.
- **Emoji:** tap **123**, then **Emoji**.
- **Delete:** tap **Delete** for one character, or hold it to keep deleting. Flick left from it to delete a word.
- **Copy and paste:** tap **Ctrl**, then **C**, **X**, **V** or **Z**.

## Move the cursor

Hold the space bar still for a moment, or slide along it. The keys go quiet and the whole keyboard moves the cursor. Lift to type again.

## Use the keys as a touchpad

Tap the mark at the right end of the space bar. The whole keyboard becomes a touchpad: move one finger to move the pointer, tap to click, and move two fingers to scroll. Tap the mark again to go back to typing.
