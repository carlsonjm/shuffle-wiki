| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Open Spread | Swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen. Or put three fingers down on a touchscreen, or four on a touchpad. |
| Open Table | Pull down from the top edge of the screen. Or push three fingers up on a touchscreen, or four on a touchpad. |
| The card before or after | Tap, or swipe inward, in the gap beside the Active card. |
| Close an app | In Spread, flick its card up. |
| Open Search | Tap the dot at the left end of the Actions Panel. |
| Bring up the on-screen keys | Tap a text field. |
| Open an app's sheet on the Actions Panel | Touch and hold its icon. |

## Swipes in from the sides

A swipe in from the left or right edge of a touchscreen can do whatever you choose. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings, choose **Shortcuts**, then **Edge swipes**.
