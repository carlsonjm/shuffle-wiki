Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> + <kbd>I</kbd> to open Shuffle Settings and choose **Keyboard, mouse and touch**.

## Type in another language

Under **Languages you type in**:

1. Tap **Add a language**, find it under **Find a language or layout**, and choose it.
2. The first language in the list is the one you start with. **Move up** changes the order, and **Remove** takes one away.
3. **Switch language with** sets the key that moves to the next language.

**A new language is used in** chooses whether a switch applies to **Every window**, **This desktop**, **This app** or **This window**. **Show the new language on screen** shows its name when you switch.

## Holding a key down

**Holding a key down** chooses what a key on a plugged-in keyboard does when you hold it: **Repeats it**, **Offers accents**, or **Does nothing**. **Wait before repeating** and **Repeat speed** set how it repeats. Try them in **Try it here**.

## The on-screen keyboard and tablet mode

Under **Touch screen and on-screen keyboard**:

- **Tablet mode** makes touch targets bigger and brings up the on-screen keys. Choose **When needed**, **Always** or **Never**.
- **On-screen keyboard** chooses the [Shuffle Keyboard](/shuffle/keys-and-touch/on-screen-keyboard/), or **None** to turn the on-screen keys off. It comes up in tablet mode, and when you tap where text goes with no keyboard plugged in.
- **Shuffle Keyboard languages** chooses the languages of the on-screen keys.
- **Pens, controllers and more** holds the settings for a touch monitor, a pen or drawing tablet, and game controllers.
