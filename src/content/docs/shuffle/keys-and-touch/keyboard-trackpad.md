## Move the text cursor

Hold the space bar still for a moment, or slide along it. The keys go quiet and the whole keyboard moves the cursor.

- Move sideways to move one character at a time.
- Move up or down to move one line at a time.
- Tap **Shift** first to select as you move, or **Ctrl** first to move by words.
- Lift to type again. The cursor stays where you left it.

It works wherever arrow keys work, terminals included.

## Use the keys as a touchpad

Tap the mark at the right end of the space bar. The keys dim and the whole keyboard becomes a touchpad.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Move the pointer | Move one finger. |
| Click | Tap one finger. |
| Select text or drag something | Hold one finger still, then move. Lift to let go. |
| Scroll | Move two fingers. |
| Right-click | Tap two fingers. |

Tap the mark again to go back to typing. **Hide** puts the keys away and ends it.

The first time, on some computers, Plasma asks once whether the keyboard may move the pointer, and shows a notice while it does.
