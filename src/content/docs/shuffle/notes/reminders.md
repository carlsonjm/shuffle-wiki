## From Search

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Type **remind me to** and what to remember, with when, such as **remind me to call the dentist tomorrow**.
3. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The note opens with the time already set.

## On a note

Open the note, tap **Remind**, and choose a time, such as **Later today**, **This evening** or **Tomorrow**. **Pick a time** lets you choose any day and hour. **Remind** then shows when. To take the reminder off, tap **Remind** and choose **No reminder**.

## When it is due

The reminder shows as a notification with the note's first words. Tap it to open the note. **Done** marks it done, and **In 10 minutes** reminds you again ten minutes later.

A reminder that comes due while the computer is asleep shows when it wakes.
