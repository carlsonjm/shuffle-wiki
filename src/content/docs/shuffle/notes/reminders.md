A reminder is a note that tells you about itself at a time you choose. It also goes on the [notes board's planner](/shuffle/notes/board/) for that day.

## From Search

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Type **remind me to** and what to remember, with when, such as **remind me to call the dentist tomorrow**.
3. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The note opens with the time already set.

## On a note

Open the note and tap **Remind**. Choose one of the times it offers:

- **Later today**, two hours from now, rounded to the hour. It shows until the evening.
- **This evening**, at 7 PM. It shows until 6 PM.
- **Tomorrow**, at 9 AM.
- **Next time this opens**, on a note [stuck to a window](/shuffle/notes/window-notes/). It reminds you the next time that document opens, so it has no day.
- **Pick a time**. Use the arrows to choose the day, and the hour or quarter hour, then tap **Set**.

**Remind** then shows when. The cross beside it takes the reminder off. Tap **Remind** again and choose another time to change it.

Naming a day in the note, such as **friday** or **tonight**, also offers a reminder just under the note. Tap the offer to set it.

## When it is due

The reminder shows as a [notification](/shuffle/around-the-screen/notifications/) with the note's first words, when it was for, and the note's window or folder.

- Tap it to open the note.
- **Done** marks the note done on the planner.
- **In 10 minutes** reminds you once more, ten minutes later.

If you miss it, it waits in the list under the bell. A reminder that comes due while the computer is asleep shows when it wakes.
