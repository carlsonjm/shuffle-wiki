A note can belong to a window, such as a document you are working on. It comes back each time that window opens.

## Stick a note

1. Open the note. See [Write and find notes](/shuffle/notes/notes/).
2. Under the note, tap the window's name, or the place where it shows one.
3. Choose the window. **Don't stick to a window** unsticks it.

## See a window's notes

While a window has notes, the quick note island on the Actions Panel shows a dot in the note's color, with a count when there are several. Tap it to bring that window's notes up over it. Tap it again to put them away.
