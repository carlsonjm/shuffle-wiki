A note can belong to a window, such as a document you are working on. It comes back each time that window opens. A note you start while a window is in front is stuck to that window to begin with.

## Stick a note, or unstick it

1. Open the note. See [Write and find notes](/shuffle/notes/notes/).
2. Under the note, tap the window's name, or **Stick to a window**. The open windows are listed, the one in front first.
3. Tap a window. The note stays in its folder too.

**Don't stick to a window** unsticks it.

## See a window's notes

While the window in front has notes, the quick note island on the Actions Panel shows a dot in the top note's colour, with a count when there are several. Tap the dot to bring the notes up over the window, each where you last left it. Tap the dot again to put them away.

- Tap a note to type in it. <kbd>Esc</kbd> stops typing and keeps what you wrote.
- Tap the window around the notes, or go to another window, and they fold into a small dot where the top note stands. Tap that dot to open them again.

## Move, resize and copy

- **Move a note:** drag it. With a mouse, drag it by its edge or the space under its words. It keeps its place when the window is resized.
- **Resize a note:** drag its bottom-right corner. It keeps that size the next time.
- **Copy words:** press and hold a word, then tap **Copy**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd>. Right-click a note for **Copy all** and **Select all**.

## In Spread

In [Spread](/shuffle/cards-and-workspaces/spread/), each card shows its notes where you left them, faintly while they are put away. Notes never placed sit in a small stack in the card's bottom-right corner.

- Tap a note, its dot or the stack to open the card with the notes up.
- Hold a note, or the stack, and let go on another card to stick it to that window instead.
