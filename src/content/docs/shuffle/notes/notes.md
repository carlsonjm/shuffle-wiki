Shuffle has its own notes. They are kept on this computer as you type, so there is nothing to save.

## Write a quick note

There are three ways to start one:

- Tap or click the **+** on the quick note island, beside Search's dot on the Actions Panel. Type your note, then tap **Done**. **Open in Notes** moves it to the larger note card.
- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, type **note:** and your words, such as **note: milk**, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, tap or click **Notes** in the row along its top, then tap **New note** in the corner.

A note closed with nothing written in it leaves nothing behind. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or tap outside the note, to finish it.

## See and find your notes

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Tap or click **Notes** in the row along its top. Your notes are listed.
3. Type in **Find a note** to find notes with those words. A folder with that name is listed under the notes.
4. Tap a filter to see only some notes: **Everything**, today's notes, **On windows**, a folder, or **Tucked away**.

Tap a note to open it, or pick one with <kbd>Down</kbd> and <kbd>Up</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. If you type words no note has and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, a new note starts with them.

**Expand**, at the right of the row, makes Search fill the screen. **Pop out** opens the [notes board](/shuffle/notes/board/), with the planner for your day.

## Change a note

- **Colour:** tap the colour dot beside the note's words, then one of the five colours.
- **Folder:** tap the folder's name under the note. See [Keep notes in folders](/shuffle/notes/folders/).
- **Window:** tap the window's name under the note to [stick it to a window](/shuffle/notes/window-notes/).
- **Checklist:** tap **Checklist** to put a box on each line. See below.
- **Reminder:** tap **Remind**. See [Set a reminder](/shuffle/notes/reminders/).
- **More:** tap **More** for **Copy all**, **Pin**, **Tuck away** and **Remove**.

## Let the note offer

Some words make the note offer something just under it. Tap the offer to accept it.

- Name a day, such as **friday**, and it offers a reminder at 9 AM that day. **Tonight** offers 6 PM.
- Start lines with **-** and a space, and it offers to make them a checklist.
- Write a **#word**, such as **#garden**, and it offers to keep the note in that folder, making the folder if it is new.

## Make a checklist

Tap **Checklist**. Each line becomes an item with a box. With more than one line, the first stays above as the list's heading.

- Tap a box to tick the item. Tap it again to untick it.
- Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> in an item to start a new one below it.
- Press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> in an empty item to remove it.
- Tap **Checklist** again to turn it back into plain text. Nothing is lost.

## Pin, tuck away and remove

- **Pin** keeps the note on the quick note island, in place of the note pinned before. Tap it there to open it. **Unpin** takes it off.
- **Tuck away** hides the note until you need it. You can still see it under the **Tucked away** filter. To put it back in its folder, open the [notes board](/shuffle/notes/board/), tap **Tucked away**, then the note. **Bring back**, in the message that follows tucking it away, undoes it at once.
- **Remove** moves the note to the Trash. **Undo**, in the message that follows, brings it back.

## Copy words from a note

Press and hold a word to select it. Slide on without lifting to take in more words, or drag across them with a mouse. Then tap **Copy**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd>. **More**, then **Copy all**, copies the whole note. **Copy** does not show on a checklist.

## What Notes does not do

Notes are for short things you want to keep in sight. They stay on this computer: there is no sync to other computers, no phone app, no account and no cloud. There is no handwriting, no rich formatting and no sharing a note with someone else. For a long document, use a word processor.
