Shuffle has its own notes. They are kept on this computer as you type.

## Write a quick note

Tap or click the **+** on the quick note island, beside Search's dot on the Actions Panel. Type your note, then tap **Done**. Or type **note:** and your words in Search, such as **note: milk**, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## See all your notes

1. Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search.
2. Tap or click **Notes** in the row along its top. Your notes are listed.
3. Type in **Find a note** to find notes with those words.
4. Tap a filter, such as a folder or **Tucked away**, to see only those notes.

Tap a note to open it. **New note**, in the corner, starts a new one.

## On a note

- **Color:** tap the color dot in the note's corner.
- **Folder:** tap the folder's name under the note, then choose a folder, or type a name for a new one.
- **Checklist:** tap **Checklist** to put a box on each line. Tap a box to tick it.
- **Reminder:** tap **Remind**. See [Set a reminder](/shuffle/notes/reminders/).
- **More:** tap **More** for **Copy all**, **Pin**, **Tuck away** and **Remove**. **Pin** keeps the note on the quick note island. **Tuck away** hides the note until you need it again, under **Tucked away**.
