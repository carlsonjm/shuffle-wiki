The **notes board** is Notes in a window of its own. It shows your notes beside a planner for the day, and every folder along its top. It opens like any other window, so it can sit beside another one as a card.

## Open the board

Press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd> to open Search, tap or click **Notes** in the row along its top, then tap **Pop out**.

## Plan the day

**Today** is the planner. It lists today's notes that have a time, then the days ahead that have any. Notes with no date sit beside them as ideas.

- Tap a day in the strip of days to see that day's notes and the days after it. A day with notes planned carries a dot.
- Tap a row to open the note.
- Tap the tick at the end of a row to mark the note done. **Done** stands where its time was. Tap the tick again to undo it.

The next note still to come today stands out. Each time you open the board, it starts on today.

A note goes on the planner when you give it a [reminder](/shuffle/notes/reminders/).

## Move a note

Press and hold a note on the board until it lifts, then let go over where it should go:

- Over a folder or **Inbox**, to keep it there.
- Over **Tucked away**, to tuck it away.
- Over a day in the strip, to plan it for that day. **Undo** takes it back off.
- Over **Trash**, which shows at the bottom while you hold a note. **Undo** brings it back.

Let go anywhere else and nothing changes.

## Find or start a note

Type in **Find or start a note** at the top. The board shows every note with those words, or in a folder or stuck to a window with that name, tucked away ones too. Clear the words to go back. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> with words no note has, and a new note starts with them in **Inbox**.

**New note** starts a note in the folder the board is showing.
