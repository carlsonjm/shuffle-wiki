Every note is kept in a folder. A new note goes into **Inbox**, or into the workspace's own folder if you have given the workspace one.

## Put a note in a folder

1. Open the note. See [Write and find notes](/shuffle/notes/notes/).
2. Under the note, tap the folder's name.
3. Tap a folder. The note is kept there at once.

To make a new folder, type its name in **New folder** and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The note goes into it. If a name cannot be used, the note says why.

You can also write a **#word** in the note, such as **#garden**, and tap the offer under it. The note goes into that folder, which is made if it is new.

## Make, rename and remove folders on the board

Open the [notes board](/shuffle/notes/board/), then:

- Tap **New folder**, at the end of the folders. Type a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- Open a folder and tap **Rename** under its name. Type the new name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Its notes stay in it.
- Tap **Remove folder**. Its notes go to **Inbox**. **Undo**, in the message that follows, puts them back.

## Give a workspace its own folder

Open a folder on the board and tap **New notes on**, followed by the workspace's name, **go here**. Notes you write on that workspace now go into this folder. Tap it again to send them to **Inbox** instead.

To see one folder's notes in Search, press <kbd><a href="/start/first-steps/meta-key/">Meta</a></kbd>, tap **Notes**, then tap the folder among the filters.
